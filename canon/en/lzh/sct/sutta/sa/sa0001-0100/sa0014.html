雜阿含經 The Related Discourses

（一）陰相應 1. The Aggregates

(一四) 味 20 (14). Enjoyment (2)

如是我聞： Thus I have heard:

一時，佛住舍衞國、祇樹、給孤獨園。 One time, the Buddha was staying at Anāthapiṇḍada’s Park in Jeta’s Grove of Śrāvastī.

爾時，世尊告諸比丘： It was then that the Bhagavān addressed the monks,

「我昔於色味有求，有行。 “In the past, I had pursued and practiced the enjoyment of form.

若於色味隨順覺，則於色味以智慧如實見。 When I realized the enjoyment of form as a result, I truly saw it with wisdom.

如是，於受⋯想⋯行⋯識味有求，有行。 Thus, I had pursued and practiced the enjoyment of feeling … conception … volition … awareness.

若於受⋯想⋯行⋯識味隨順覺，則於識味以智慧如實見。 When I realized the enjoyment of feeling … conception … volition … awareness as a result, I truly saw it with wisdom.

「諸比丘，我於色患有求，有行。 “Monks, I pursued and practiced the trouble of form.

若於色患隨順覺，則於色患以智慧如實見。 When I realized form’s trouble as a result, I truly saw it with wisdom.

如是，受⋯想⋯行⋯識患有求，有行。 Thus, I pursued and practiced the trouble of feeling … conception … volition … awareness.

若於識患隨順覺，則於識患以智慧如實見。 When I realized the trouble of awareness as a result, I truly saw it with wisdom.

「諸比丘，我於色離有求，有行。 “Monks, I pursued and practiced the escape from form.

若於色離隨順覺，則於色離以智慧如實見。 When I realized the escape from form as a result, I truly saw it with wisdom.

如是，受⋯想⋯行⋯識離有求，有行。 Thus, I pursued and practiced the escape from feeling … conception … volition … awareness.

若於受⋯想⋯行⋯識離隨順覺，則於受⋯想⋯行⋯識離以智慧如實見。 When I realized the escape from awareness as a result, I truly saw it with wisdom.

「諸比丘，我於五受陰不如實知味是味⋯患是患⋯離是離者， “Monks, had I not truly known the enjoyment of the five acquired aggregates to be enjoyment … trouble to be trouble … escape to be escape, I wouldn’t be freed, escaped, and parted with them.

我於諸天，若魔、若梵，沙門、婆羅門，天、人眾中不脫，不離，不出，永住顛倒，不能自證得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。 I would have forever stood in delusion among gods like Māra or Brahmā, ascetics and priests, or the assemblies of gods and humans, and I couldn’t have realized my attainment of the unsurpassed and complete awakening.

「諸比丘，我以如實知五受陰味是味⋯患是患⋯離是離，我於諸天若魔、若梵，沙門、婆羅門，天、人眾中以脫，以離，以出，永不住顛倒。 “Monks, it’s because I truly knew the enjoyment of the five acquired aggregates to be enjoyment … trouble to be trouble … escape to be escape that I was freed, escaped, parted, and liberated from bondage.

能自證得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。 I never again stood in delusion among gods like Māra or Brahmā, ascetics and priests, or the assemblies of gods and humans, and I could realize my attainment of the unsurpassed and complete awakening.”

時，諸比丘聞佛所說歡喜，奉行。 When the monks heard what the Buddha taught, they rejoiced and approved.

Summary Verse

過去四種說、 [11-14] The past explained in four ways,

厭離、及解脫、 [15] Disillusionment and [16] liberation,

二種說因縁、 [17-18] Two ways of explaining causes and conditions,

味亦復二種。 And [19-20] two kinds of enjoyment.
