PuṇṇānumodanaṁRejoicing in Merits

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā devā nāgā mahiddhikā,May those powerful gods and nāgas stationed in the sky or on the earth,

puññaṁ taṁ anumoditvā ciraṁ rakkhantu sāsanaṁ!having shared in this merit protect the teaching for a long time!

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā devā nāgā mahiddhikā,May those powerful gods and nāgas stationed in the sky or on the earth,

puññaṁ taṁ anumoditvā ciraṁ rakkhantu desanaṁ!having shared in this merit protect the preaching for a long time!

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā devā nāgā mahiddhikā,May those powerful gods and nāgas stationed in the sky or on the earth,

puññaṁ taṁ anumoditvā ciraṁ rakkhantu maṁ paran!-tihaving shared in this merit protect me and others for a long time!

Devo vassatu kālena, sabbasampatti hetu ca,May the sky-god rain down in due season, and (may there be) the right conditions for all good fortune,

phīto bhavatu loko ca, rājā bhavatu dhammiko!may the world be prosperous, may the king always be righteous.
