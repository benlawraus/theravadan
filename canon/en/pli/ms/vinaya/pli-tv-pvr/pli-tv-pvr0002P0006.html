Bhikkhunivibhaṅga The Nuns’ Analysis

Paṭhamabhāga Part one

2.6 Adhikaraṇavāra The legal issues to which each offense belongs

Avassutāya bhikkhuniyā avassutassa purisapuggalassa kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyantiyā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ katamaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ? When it comes to the offenses for a lustful nun consenting to a lustful man making physical contact with her, to which of the four kinds of legal issues do they belong?

Avassutāya bhikkhuniyā avassutassa purisapuggalassa kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyantiyā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ—āpattādhikaraṇaṁ …pe…. They belong to legal issues arising from an offense. …

Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjantiyā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ katamaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ? When it comes to the offenses for asking for curd and then eating it, to which of the four kinds of legal issues do they belong?

Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjantiyā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ—āpattādhikaraṇaṁ. They belong to legal issues arising from an offense.

Adhikaraṇavāro niṭṭhito chaṭṭho. The legal issues to which each offense belongs, the sixth, are finished.
