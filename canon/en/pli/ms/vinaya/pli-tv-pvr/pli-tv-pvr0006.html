6. Khandhakapucchāvāra The classes of offenses in the Chapters

Upasampadaṁ pucchissaṁ sanidānaṁ saniddesaṁ, “I will ask about the Full Ordination, together with its origin stories and its detailed explanations:

Samukkaṭṭhapadānaṁ kati āpattiyo; how many kinds of offenses are laid down in its exalted clauses?

Upasampadaṁ vissajjissaṁ sanidānaṁ saniddesaṁ, I will answer about the Full Ordination, together with its origin stories and its detailed explanations:

Samukkaṭṭhapadānaṁ dve āpattiyo. two kinds of offenses are laid down in its exalted clauses.

Tassuddānaṁ This is the summary:

Upasampadūposatho, “Full ordination, observance day,

Vassūpanāyikapavāraṇā; Entering the rainy-season residence, invitation ceremony;

Cammabhesajjakathinā, Skins, medicines, robe-making ceremony,

Cīvaraṁ campeyyakena ca. Robes, and with legal procedures,

Kosambakkhandhakaṁ kammaṁ, The chapter on division in the Sangha, legal procedures,

Pārivāsisamuccayā; The proper conduct for those who have committed an offense entailing suspension, legal procedures in relation to offenses entailing suspension;

Samathakhuddakā senā, Settling of legal issues, minor topics, resting places,

Saṅghabhedaṁ samācāro; Schism in the Sangha, proper conduct;

Ṭhapanaṁ bhikkhunikkhandhaṁ, Cancellation of the Monastic code, the chapter on nuns,

Pañcasattasatena cāti. And with the first council and the second council.”
