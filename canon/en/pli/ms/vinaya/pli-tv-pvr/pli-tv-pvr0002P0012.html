Bhikkhunivibhaṅga The Nuns’ Analysis

Dutiyabhāga Part two

2.12 Saṅgahavāra The classes of offenses to which each rule belongs

Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ katihi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā? When it comes to the offenses that are a result of consenting to physical contact, in how many of the seven classes of offenses are they found?

Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ pañcahi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā—They are found in five:

siyā pārājikāpattikkhandhena, siyā saṅghādisesāpattikkhandhena, siyā thullaccayāpattikkhandhena, siyā pācittiyāpattikkhandhena, siyā dukkaṭāpattikkhandhena …pe…. they may be in the class of offenses entailing expulsion; they may be in the class of offenses entailing suspension; they may be in the class of serious offenses; they may be in the class of offenses entailing confession; they may be in the class of offenses of wrong conduct. …

Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ katihi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā? When it comes to the offenses that are a result of asking for curd and then eating it, in how many of the seven classes of offenses are they found?

Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ dvīhi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā—They are found in two:

siyā pāṭidesanīyāpattikkhandhena, siyā dukkaṭāpattikkhandhena. they may be in the class of offenses entailing acknowledgment; they may be in the class of offenses of wrong conduct.

Saṅgahavāro niṭṭhito catuttho. The classes of offenses to which each rule belongs, the fourth, are finished.
