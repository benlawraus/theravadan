Bhikkhunivibhaṅga The Nuns’ Analysis

Adhikaraṇasamatha The settling of legal issues

Ime kho panāyyāyo satta adhikaraṇasamathā dhammā uddesaṁ āgacchanti. “Venerables, these seven principles for the settling of legal issues come up for recitation.

Uppannuppannānaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ samathāya vūpasamāya For the settling and resolving of legal issues whenever they arise there is:

sammukhāvinayo dātabbo, Resolution face-to-face to be applied;
