Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.268 Numbered Discourses 4.268

27. Kammapathavagga 27. Ways of Performing Deeds

Pisuṇavācāsutta Divisive Speech

…pe… Attanā ca pisuṇavāco hoti, parañca pisuṇāya vācāya samādapeti, pisuṇāya vācāya ca samanuñño hoti, pisuṇāya vācāya ca vaṇṇaṁ bhāsati—imehi …pe…. “… They themselves speak divisively …

Attanā ca pisuṇāya vācāya paṭivirato hoti, parañca pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇiyā samādapeti, pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇiyā ca samanuñño hoti, pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇiyā ca vaṇṇaṁ bhāsati—imehi …pe…. … They themselves don’t speak divisively …”

Pañcamaṁ.
