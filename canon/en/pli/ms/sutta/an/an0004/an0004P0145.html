Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.145 Numbered Discourses 4.145

15. Ābhāvagga 15. Brightness

Pajjotasutta Lamps

“Cattārome, bhikkhave, pajjotā. “Mendicants, there are these four lamps.

Katame cattāro? What four?

Candapajjoto, sūriyapajjoto, aggipajjoto, paññāpajjoto—The lamps of the moon, sun, fire, and wisdom.

Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro pajjotā. These are the four lamps.

Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ catunnaṁ pajjotānaṁ yadidaṁ paññāpajjoto”ti. The best of these four lamps is the lamp of wisdom.”

Pañcamaṁ.
