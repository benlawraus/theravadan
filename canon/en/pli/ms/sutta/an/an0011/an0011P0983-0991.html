Aṅguttara Nikāya 11 Numbered Discourses 11.983–991

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka The First Fifty

Rāgapeyyāla Abbreviated Texts Beginning with Greed

~ Untitled Discourses on Greed

“Rāgassa, bhikkhave, pariññāya… “For the complete understanding of greed …

parikkhayāya… complete ending …

pahānāya… giving up …

khayāya… ending …

vayāya… vanishing …

virāgāya… fading away …

nirodhāya… cessation …

cāgāya… giving away …

paṭinissaggāya… letting go …

ime ekādasa dhammā bhāvetabbā”. these eleven things should be developed.”
