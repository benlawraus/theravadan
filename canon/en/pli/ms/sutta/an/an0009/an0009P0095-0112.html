Aṅguttara Nikāya 9 Numbered Discourses 9

10. Rāgapeyyāla 10. Abbreviated Texts Beginning with Greed

~ Untitled Discourses on Greed

“Rāgassa, bhikkhave, pariññāya …pe… “For the complete understanding of greed …

parikkhayāya …pe… complete ending …

pahānāya …pe… giving up …

khayāya …pe… ending …

vayāya …pe… vanishing …

virāgāya …pe… fading away …

nirodhāya …pe… cessation …

cāgāya …pe… giving away …

paṭinissaggāya …pe… letting go of greed …

ime nava dhammā bhāvetabbā”. these nine things should be developed.”
