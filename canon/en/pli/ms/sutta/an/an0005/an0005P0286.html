Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.286 Numbered Discourses 5.286

28. Sikkhāpadapeyyāla 28. Abbreviated Texts on Training Rules

Bhikkhusutta A Monk

“Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. “Mendicants, a monk with five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.

Katamehi pañcahi? What five?

Pāṇātipātī hoti, adinnādāyī hoti, abrahmacārī hoti, musāvādī hoti, surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhāyī hoti. He kills living creatures, steals, has sex, lies, and consumes intoxicants of beer, wine, and liquor.

Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. A monk with these five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.

Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge. A monk with five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.

Katamehi pañcahi? What five?

Pāṇātipātā paṭivirato hoti, adinnādānā paṭivirato hoti, abrahmacariyā paṭivirato hoti, musāvādā paṭivirato hoti, surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā paṭivirato hoti. He doesn’t kill living creatures, steal, have sex, lie, or consume intoxicants of beer, wine, and liquor.

Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge”ti. A monk with these five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”

Paṭhamaṁ.
