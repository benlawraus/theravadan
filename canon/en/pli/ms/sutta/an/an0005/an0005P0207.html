Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.207 Numbered Discourses 5.207

21. Kimilavagga 21. With Kimbila

Yāgusutta Porridge

“Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā yāguyā. “Mendicants, there are these five benefits of porridge.

Katame pañca? What five?

Khuddaṁ paṭihanati, pipāsaṁ paṭivineti, vātaṁ anulometi, vatthiṁ sodheti, āmāvasesaṁ pāceti. It wards off hunger, quenches thirst, settles the wind, cleans the bladder, and helps digestion.

Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā yāguyā”ti. These are the five benefits of porridge.”

Sattamaṁ.
