Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.47 Numbered Discourses 3.47Numbered Discourses 3.47

5. Cūḷavagga 5. The Lesser Chapter 5. The Lesser Chapter

Saṅkhatalakkhaṇasutta Fabricated Characteristics of the Conditioned

“Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, saṅkhatassa saṅkhatalakkhaṇāni. “Monks, these three are fabricated characteristics of what is fabricated. “Mendicants, the conditioned has these three characteristics.

Katamāni tīṇi? Which three? What three?

Uppādo paññāyati, vayo paññāyati, ṭhitassa aññathattaṁ paññāyati. Arising is discernable, passing away is discernable, alteration [literally: otherness] while staying is discernable. Arising is evident, vanishing is evident, and change while persisting is evident.

Imāni kho, bhikkhave, tīṇi saṅkhatassa saṅkhatalakkhaṇānī”ti. “These are three fabricated characteristics of what is fabricated.” These are the three characteristics of the conditioned.”

Sattamaṁ.

Asaṅkhatalakkhaṇasutta Characteristics of the Unconditioned

“Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, asaṅkhatassa asaṅkhatalakkhaṇāni. “Now these three are unfabricated characteristics of what is unfabricated. “The unconditioned has these three characteristics.

Katamāni tīṇi? Which three? What three?

Na uppādo paññāyati, na vayo paññāyati, na ṭhitassa aññathattaṁ paññāyati. No arising is discernable, no passing away is discernable, no alteration while staying is discernable. No arising is evident, no vanishing is evident, and no change while persisting is evident.

Imāni kho, bhikkhave, tīṇi asaṅkhatassa asaṅkhatalakkhaṇānī”ti. “These are three unfabricated characteristics of what is unfabricated.” These are the three characteristics of the unconditioned.”

Aṭṭhamaṁ.
