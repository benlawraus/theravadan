Aṅguttara Nikāya 6 Numbered Discourses 6.143–169

13. Rāgapeyyāla 13. Abbreviated Texts Beginning with Greed

~ Untitled Discourses on Greed, Etc.

“Rāgassa, bhikkhave, pariññāya …pe… “For the complete understanding of greed …

parikkhayāya … complete ending …

pahānāya … giving up …

khayāya … ending …

vayāya … vanishing …

virāgāya … fading away …

nirodhāya … cessation …

cāgāya … giving away …

paṭinissaggāya cha dhammā bhāvetabbā”. letting go of greed these six things should be developed.”
