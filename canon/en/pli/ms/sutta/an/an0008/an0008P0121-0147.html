Aṅguttara Nikāya 8 Numbered Discourses 8.121–147

11. Rāgapeyyāla 11. Abbreviated Texts Beginning With Greed

~ Untitled Discourses on Greed

“Rāgassa, bhikkhave, pariññāya …pe… “For the complete understanding of greed …

parikkhayāya … complete ending …

pahānāya … giving up …

khayāya … ending …

vayāya … vanishing …

virāgāya … fading away …

nirodhāya … cessation …

cāgāya … giving away …

paṭinissaggāya …pe… letting go …

ime aṭṭha dhammā bhāvetabbā”. these eight things should be developed.”
