Saṁyutta Nikāya 37.28 Linked Discourses 37.28

3. Balavagga 3. Powers

Ekasutta One

“Ekena ca kho, bhikkhave, balena samannāgato puriso mātugāmaṁ abhibhuyya vattati. “Mendicants, by means of one power a man lives having mastered a lady.

Katamena ekena balena? By means of what one power?

Issariyabalena abhibhūtaṁ mātugāmaṁ neva rūpabalaṁ tāyati, na bhogabalaṁ tāyati, na ñātibalaṁ tāyati, na puttabalaṁ tāyati, na sīlabalaṁ tāyatī”ti. When a lady has been mastered by the power of authority, her powers of attractiveness, wealth, relatives, children, and ethical behavior do not avail her.”

Catutthaṁ.
