Saṁyutta Nikāya 40.11 Linked Discourses 40.11

1. Moggallānavagga 1. By Moggallāna

Candanasutta With Candana, Etc.

Atha kho candano devaputto …pe…. Then the godling Candana …

Atha kho suyāmo devaputto …pe…. the godling Suyāma …

Atha kho santusito devaputto …pe…. the godling Santusita …

Atha kho sunimmito devaputto …pe…. the godling Sunimmita …

Atha kho vasavatti devaputto …pe…. the godling Vasavatti …

(Yathā sakkasuttaṁ tathā ime pañca peyyālā vitthāretabbā.) (Tell in full as in the Discourse With Sakka.)

Ekādasamaṁ.

Moggallānavaggo paṭhamo.

Tassuddānaṁ

Savitakkāvitakkañca,

sukhena ca upekkhako;

Ākāsañceva viññāṇaṁ,

ākiñcaṁ nevasaññinā;

Animitto ca sakko ca,

candanekādasena cāti.

Moggallānasaṁyuttaṁ samattaṁ. The Linked Discourses on Moggallāna are complete.
