Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.70 Linked Discourses 1.70

7. Addhavagga 7. Overcome

Lokasutta The World World

“Kismiṁ loko samuppanno, “In what has the world arisen? “In what has the world arisen?

kismiṁ kubbati santhavaṁ; With what does it get intimate? In what does it form intimacy?

Kissa loko upādāya, By grasping what By clinging to what is the world

kismiṁ loko vihaññatī”ti. is the world troubled in what?” Harassed in regard to what?”

“Chasu loko samuppanno, “The world has arisen in six. “In six has the world arisen;

chasu kubbati santhavaṁ; It gets intimate with six. In six it forms intimacy;

Channameva upādāya, Derived from the six, By clinging to six the world

chasu loko vihaññatī”ti. the world’s troubled in six.” Is harassed in regard to six. ”

Addhavaggo sattamo.

Tassuddānaṁ

Nāmaṁ cittañca taṇhā ca,

saṁyojanañca bandhanā;

Abbhāhatuḍḍito pihito,

icchā lokena te dasāti.
