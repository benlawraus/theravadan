Dīghanakhasutta With Dīghanakha

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—Thus I heard:So I have heard.

Seyyathāpi, bho gotama, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evameva kho bhotā gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito. As though he were righting the overthrown, revealing the hidden, showing the way to one who was lost, holding up a lamp in the darkness so that those with eyes could see forms—so too the Dhamma has been made clear in many ways by Master Gotama.As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, worthy Gotama has made the teaching clear in many ways.

Esāhaṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca. I go to Master Gotama for refuge, and to the Dhamma, and to the Sangha of bhikkhus.I go for refuge to the worthy Gotama, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti. Let Master Gotama remember me as a lay follower who has gone to him for refuge from today for life.”From this day forth, may the worthy Gotama remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.”

Dīghanakhasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ catutthaṁ.
