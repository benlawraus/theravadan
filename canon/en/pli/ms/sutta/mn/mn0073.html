Majjhima Nikāya 73 Middle Discourses 73

Mahāvacchasutta The Longer Discourse With Vacchagotta

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—Thus I heard:So I have heard.

ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati veḷuvane kalandakanivāpe. On one occasion the Blessed One was living at Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the Squirrels’ Sanctuary.At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the squirrels’ feeding ground.

Atha kho vacchagotto paribbājako yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi. Then the Wanderer Vacchagotta went to the Blessed One and exchanged greetings with him.Then the wanderer Vacchagotta went up to the Buddha and exchanged greetings with him.

Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho vacchagotto paribbājako bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: When the courteous and amiable talk was finished, he sat down at one side. When he had done so, he said to the Blessed One:When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side and said to the Buddha,

“dīgharattāhaṁ bhotā gotamena sahakathī. “I have had conversations with Master Gotama for a long time.“For a long time I have had discussions with the worthy Gotama.

Sādhu me bhavaṁ gotamo saṅkhittena kusalākusalaṁ desetū”ti. It would be good if Master Gotama would show me the profitable and the unprofitable in brief.”Please teach me in brief what is skillful and what is unskillful.”

“Saṅkhittenapi kho te ahaṁ, vaccha, kusalākusalaṁ deseyyaṁ, vitthārenapi kho te ahaṁ, vaccha, kusalākusalaṁ deseyyaṁ; “I could show you the profitable and the unprofitable in brief, Vaccha, and I could show you the profitable and the unprofitable in detail;“Vaccha, I can teach you what is skillful and what is unskillful in brief or in detail.

api ca te ahaṁ, vaccha, saṅkhittena kusalākusalaṁ desessāmi. but I will show you them in brief.Still, let me do so in brief.

Taṁ suṇāhi, sādhukaṁ manasi karohi, bhāsissāmī”ti. Listen and attend closely to what I shall say.”Listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

“Evaṁ, bho”ti kho vacchagotto paribbājako bhagavato paccassosi. “Yes, sir,” Vacchagotta the Wanderer replied.“Yes, worthy sir,” Vaccha replied.

Bhagavā etadavoca: The Blessed One said this:The Buddha said this:

“Lobho kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, alobho kusalaṁ; “Greed, Vaccha, is unprofitable, non-greed is profitable;“Greed is unskillful, contentment is skillful.

doso kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, adoso kusalaṁ; hate is unprofitable, non-hate is profitable;Hate is unskillful, love is skillful.

moho kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, amoho kusalaṁ. delusion is unprofitable, non-delusion is profitable.Delusion is unskillful, understanding is skillful.

Iti kho, vaccha, ime tayo dhammā akusalā, tayo dhammā kusalā. So, Vaccha, these three things are unprofitable and three things are profitable.So there are these three unskillful things and three that are skillful.

Pāṇātipāto kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, pāṇātipātā veramaṇī kusalaṁ; Killing living beings is unprofitable, abstention from killing living beings is profitable;Killing living creatures, stealing, and sexual misconduct; speech that’s false, backbiting, harsh, or nonsensical; covetousness, ill will, and wrong view: these things are unskillful.

adinnādānaṁ kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, adinnādānā veramaṇī kusalaṁ; taking what is not given is unprofitable, abstention from taking what is not given is profitable;Refraining from killing living creatures, stealing, and sexual misconduct; refraining from speech that’s false, divisive, harsh, or nonsensical; contentment, good will, and right view: these things are skillful.

kāmesumicchācāro kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, kāmesumicchācārā veramaṇī kusalaṁ; misconduct in sensual desires is unprofitable, abstention from misconduct in sensual desires is profitable;

musāvādo kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, musāvādā veramaṇī kusalaṁ; false speech is unprofitable, abstention from false speech is profitable;

pisuṇā vācā kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇī kusalaṁ; malicious speech is unprofitable, abstention from malicious speech is profitable;

pharusā vācā kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, pharusāya vācāya veramaṇī kusalaṁ; harsh speech is unprofitable, abstention from harsh speech is profitable;

samphappalāpo kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, samphappalāpā veramaṇī kusalaṁ; gossip is unprofitable, abstention from gossip is profitable;

abhijjhā kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, anabhijjhā kusalaṁ; covetousness is unprofitable, uncovetousness is profitable;

byāpādo kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ, abyāpādo kusalaṁ; ill will is unprofitable, non-ill-will is profitable;

micchādiṭṭhi kho, vaccha, akusalaṁ sammādiṭṭhi kusalaṁ. wrong view is unprofitable, right view is profitable.

Iti kho, vaccha, ime dasa dhammā akusalā, dasa dhammā kusalā. So, Vaccha, these ten things are unprofitable and ten things are profitable.So there are these ten unskillful things and ten that are skillful.

Yato kho, vaccha, bhikkhuno taṇhā pahīnā hoti ucchinnamūlā tālāvatthukatā anabhāvaṅkatā āyatiṁ anuppādadhammā, so hoti bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto”ti. When, Vaccha, a bhikkhu’s craving has been abandoned, cut off at the root, made like a palm stump, done away with, no more subject to future arising, that bhikkhu is an Arahant with taints destroyed, who has lived the life, done what was to be done, laid down the burden, reached his own goal, destroyed the fetters of being, and is completely liberated through final knowledge.”When a mendicant has given up craving so it is cut off at the root, made like a palm stump, obliterated, and unable to arise in the future, that mendicant is perfected. They’ve ended the defilements, completed the spiritual journey, done what had to be done, laid down the burden, achieved their heart’s goal, utterly ended the fetter of continued existence, and are rightly freed through enlightenment.”

“Tiṭṭhatu bhavaṁ gotamo. “Let Master Gotama’s own case rest.“Leaving aside the worthy Gotama,

Atthi pana te bhoto gotamassa ekabhikkhupi sāvako yo āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharatī”ti? Is there any one bhikkhu, a disciple of Master Gotama’s, who by realizing for himself with direct knowledge here and now enters upon and abides in the deliverance of mind and the deliverance by wisdom that are taintless with the exhaustion of the taints?”is there even a single monk disciple of Worthy Gotama who has realized the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom, and lives having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements?”

“Na kho, vaccha, ekaṁyeva sataṁ na dve satāni na tīṇi satāni na cattāri satāni na pañca satāni, atha kho bhiyyova ye bhikkhū mama sāvakā āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharantī”ti. “Not just one hundred, Vaccha, nor two hundred, nor three hundred, nor four hundred, nor five hundred, but far more are the bhikkhus, my disciples, who by realizing for themselves with direct knowledge here and now enter upon and abide in the deliverance of mind and the deliverance by wisdom that are taintless with the exhaustion of the taints.”“There are not just one hundred such monks who are my disciples, Vaccha, or two or three or four or five hundred, but many more than that.”

“Tiṭṭhatu bhavaṁ gotamo, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhū. “Let Master Gotama’s own case rest, and let the bhikkhus’ case rest.“Leaving aside the worthy Gotama and the monks,

Atthi pana bhoto gotamassa ekā bhikkhunīpi sāvikā yā āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharatī”ti? Is there any one bhikkhunī, a disciple of Master Gotama’s, who by realizing for herself with direct knowledge here and now enters upon and abides in the deliverance of mind and the deliverance by wisdom that are taintless with the exhaustion of the taints?”is there even a single nun disciple of Worthy Gotama who has realized the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom, and lives having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements?”

“Na kho, vaccha, ekaṁyeva sataṁ na dve satāni na tīṇi satāni na cattāri satāni na pañca satāni, atha kho bhiyyova yā bhikkhuniyo mama sāvikā āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharantī”ti. “Not just one hundred, Vaccha, nor two hundred, nor three hundred, nor four hundred, nor five hundred, but far more are the bhikkhunīs, my disciples, who by realizing for themselves with direct knowledge here and now enter upon and abide in the deliverance of mind and the deliverance by wisdom that are taintless with the exhaustion of the taints.”“There are not just one hundred such nuns who are my disciples, Vaccha, or two or three or four or five hundred, but many more than that.”

“Tiṭṭhatu bhavaṁ gotamo, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhū, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhuniyo. “Let Master Gotama’s own case rest, and let the bhikkhus’ and bhikkhunīs’ case rest.“Leaving aside the worthy Gotama, the monks, and the nuns,

Atthi pana bhoto gotamassa ekupāsakopi sāvako gihī odātavasano brahmacārī yo pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātiko tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā”ti? Is there any one lay follower, a disciple of Master Gotama’s, a householder clothed in white, living the life divine, who with the destruction of the five lower fetters will reappear spontaneously and there attain final Nibbāna without ever returning from that world?”is there even a single layman disciple of Worthy Gotama—white-clothed and chaste—who, with the ending of the five lower fetters, is reborn spontaneously, to be extinguished there, not liable to return from that world?”

“Na kho, vaccha, ekaṁyeva sataṁ na dve satāni na tīṇi satāni na cattāri satāni na pañca satāni, atha kho bhiyyova ye upāsakā mama sāvakā gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātikā tattha parinibbāyino anāvattidhammā tasmā lokā”ti. “Not just one hundred, Vaccha, nor two hundred, nor three hundred, nor four hundred, nor five hundred, but far more are the lay followers, my disciples, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, who with the destruction of the five lower fetters will reappear spontaneously and there attain final Nibbāna without ever returning from that world.”“There are not just one hundred such chaste laymen who are my disciples, Vaccha, or two or three or four or five hundred, but many more than that.”

“Tiṭṭhatu bhavaṁ gotamo, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhū, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhuniyo, tiṭṭhantu upāsakā gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino. “Let Master Gotama’s own case rest, let the bhikkhus’ and bhikkhunīs’ case rest, and let the case of the lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, rest.“Leaving aside Worthy Gotama, the monks, the nuns, and the chaste laymen,

Atthi pana bhoto gotamassa ekupāsakopi sāvako gihī odātavasano kāmabhogī sāsanakaro ovādappaṭikaro yo tiṇṇavicikiccho vigatakathaṅkatho vesārajjappatto aparappaccayo satthusāsane viharatī”ti? Is there any one lay follower, a disciple of Master Gotama’s, a householder clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, who carries out his instruction, responds to his advice, has crossed beyond doubt, is free from perplexity, has gained intrepidity, and become independent of others in the Teacher’s dispensation?”is there even a single layman disciple of the worthy Gotama—white-clothed, enjoying sensual pleasures, following instructions, and responding to advice—who has gone beyond doubt, got rid of indecision, and lives self-assured and independent of others regarding the Teacher’s instruction?”

“Na kho, vaccha, ekaṁyeva sataṁ na dve satāni na tīṇi satāni na cattāri satāni na pañca satāni, atha kho bhiyyova ye upāsakā mama sāvakā gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino sāsanakarā ovādappaṭikarā tiṇṇavicikicchā vigatakathaṅkathā vesārajjappattā aparappaccayā satthusāsane viharantī”ti. “Not just one hundred, Vaccha, nor two hundred, nor three hundred, nor four hundred, nor five hundred, but far more are the lay followers, my disciples, householders clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, who carry out my instruction, respond to my advice, have crossed beyond doubt, are free from perplexity, have gained intrepidity, and become independent of others in the Teacher’s dispensation.”“There are not just one hundred such laymen enjoying sensual pleasures who are my disciples, Vaccha, or two or three or four or five hundred, but many more than that.”

“Tiṭṭhatu bhavaṁ gotamo, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhū, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhuniyo, tiṭṭhantu upāsakā gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino, tiṭṭhantu upāsakā gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino. “Let Master Gotama’s own case rest, let the bhikkhus’ and bhikkhunīs’ case rest, and let the case of the lay followers, householders clothed in white, both those living the life divine and those enjoying sensual pleasures, rest.“Leaving aside Worthy Gotama, the monks, the nuns, the chaste laymen, and the laymen enjoying sensual pleasures,

Atthi pana bhoto gotamassa ekupāsikāpi sāvikā gihinī odātavasanā brahmacārinī yā pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātikā tattha parinibbāyinī anāvattidhammā tasmā lokā”ti? Is there any one female lay follower, a disciple of Master Gotama’s, a housewife clothed in white, living the life divine, who with the destruction of the five lower fetters will reappear spontaneously and there attain final Nibbāna without ever returning from that world?”is there even a single laywoman disciple of Worthy Gotama—white-clothed and chaste—who, with the ending of the five lower fetters, is reborn spontaneously, to be extinguished there, not liable to return from that world?”

“Na kho, vaccha, ekaṁyeva sataṁ na dve satāni na tīṇi satāni na cattāri satāni na pañca satāni, atha kho bhiyyova yā upāsikā mama sāvikā gihiniyo odātavasanā brahmacāriniyo pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātikā tattha parinibbāyiniyo anāvattidhammā tasmā lokā”ti. “Not just one hundred, Vaccha, nor two hundred, nor three hundred, nor four hundred, nor five hundred, but far more are the female lay followers, my disciples, housewives clothed in white, living the life divine, who with the destruction of the five lower fetters will reappear spontaneously and there attain final Nibbāna without ever returning from that world.”“There are not just one hundred such chaste laywomen who are my disciples, Vaccha, or two or three or four or five hundred, but many more than that.”

“Tiṭṭhatu bhavaṁ gotamo, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhū, tiṭṭhantu bhikkhuniyo, tiṭṭhantu upāsakā gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino, tiṭṭhantu upāsakā gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino, tiṭṭhantu upāsikā gihiniyo odātavasanā brahmacāriniyo. “Let Master Gotama’s own case rest, let the bhikkhus’ and bhikkhunīs’ case rest, let the case of the lay followers, householders clothed in white, both those living the life divine and those enjoying sensual pleasures, rest, and let the case of the female lay followers, housewives clothed in white, living the life divine, rest.“Leaving aside Worthy Gotama, the monks, the nuns, the chaste laymen, the laymen enjoying sensual pleasures, and the chaste laywomen,

Atthi pana bhoto gotamassa ekupāsikāpi sāvikā gihinī odātavasanā kāmabhoginī sāsanakarā ovādappaṭikarā yā tiṇṇavicikicchā vigatakathaṅkathā vesārajjappattā aparappaccayā satthusāsane viharatī”ti? Is there any one female lay follower, a disciple of Master Gotama’s, a housewife clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, who carries out his instruction, responds to his advice, has crossed beyond doubt, is free from perplexity, has gained intrepidity, and become independent of others in the Teacher’s dispensation?”is there even a single laywoman disciple of the worthy Gotama—white-clothed, enjoying sensual pleasures, following instructions, and responding to advice—who has gone beyond doubt, got rid of indecision, and lives self-assured and independent of others regarding the Teacher’s instruction?”

“Na kho, vaccha, ekaṁyeva sataṁ na dve satāni na tīṇi satāni na cattāri satāni na pañca satāni, atha kho bhiyyova yā upāsikā mama sāvikā gihiniyo odātavasanā kāmabhoginiyo sāsanakarā ovādappaṭikarā tiṇṇavicikicchā vigatakathaṅkathā vesārajjappattā aparappaccayā satthusāsane viharantī”ti. “Not just one hundred, Vaccha, nor two hundred, nor three hundred, nor four hundred, nor five hundred, but far more are the female lay followers, my disciples, housewives clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, who carry out my instruction, respond to my advice, have crossed beyond doubt, are free from perplexity, have gained intrepidity, and become independent of others in the Teacher’s dispensation.”“There are not just one hundred such laywomen enjoying sensual pleasures who are my disciples, Vaccha, or two or three or four or five hundred, but many more than that.”

“Sace hi, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavaṁyeva gotamo ārādhako abhavissa, no ca kho bhikkhū ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu; “If, Master Gotama, only Master Gotama were accomplished in this Dhamma, and no bhikkhus were accomplished in it,“If the worthy Gotama was the only one to succeed in this teaching, not any monks,

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ aparipūraṁ abhavissa tenaṅgena. then this life divine would be incomplete in that respect.then this spiritual path would be incomplete in that respect.

Yasmā ca kho, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako bhikkhū ca ārādhakā; But since Master Gotama is accomplished in this Dhamma and the bhikkhus too are accomplished in it,But because both the worthy Gotama and monks have succeeded in this teaching,

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ paripūraṁ tenaṅgena. this life divine is thus complete in that respect.this spiritual path is complete in that respect.

Sace hi, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako abhavissa, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, no ca kho bhikkhuniyo ārādhikā abhavissaṁsu; If Master Gotama and the bhikkhus were accomplished in this Dhamma, and no bhikkhunīs were accomplished in it,If the worthy Gotama and the monks were the only ones to succeed in this teaching, not any nuns …

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ aparipūraṁ abhavissa tenaṅgena. then this life divine would be incomplete in that respect.

Yasmā ca kho, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā; But since Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs are accomplished in this Dhamma,

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ paripūraṁ tenaṅgena. this life divine is thus complete in that respect.

Sace hi, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako abhavissa, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā abhavissaṁsu, no ca kho upāsakā gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu; If Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs were accomplished in this Dhamma, and no lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, were accomplished in it,chaste laymen …

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ aparipūraṁ abhavissa tenaṅgena. then this life divine would be incomplete in that respect.

Yasmā ca kho, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino ārādhakā; But since Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, are accomplished in this Dhamma,

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ paripūraṁ tenaṅgena. this life divine is thus complete in that respect.

Sace hi, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako abhavissa, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā abhavissaṁsu, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, no ca kho upāsakā gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu; If Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, were accomplished in this Dhamma, and no lay followers, householders clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, were accomplished in it,laymen enjoying sensual pleasures …

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ aparipūraṁ abhavissa tenaṅgena. then this life divine would be incomplete in that respect.

Yasmā ca kho, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino ārādhakā, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino ārādhakā; But since Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, are accomplished in this Dhamma,

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ paripūraṁ tenaṅgena. this life divine is thus complete in that respect.

Sace hi, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako abhavissa, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā abhavissaṁsu, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino ārādhakā abhavissaṁsu, no ca kho upāsikā gihiniyo odātavasanā brahmacāriniyo ārādhikā abhavissaṁsu; If Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, were accomplished in this Dhamma, and no female lay followers, housewives clothed in white, living the life divine, were accomplished in it,chaste laywomen …

evamidaṁ brahmacariyaṁ aparipūraṁ abhavissa tenaṅgena. then this life divine would be incomplete in that respect.

Yasmā ca kho, bho gotama, imaṁ dhammaṁ bhavañceva gotamo ārādhako, bhikkhū ca ārādhakā, bhikkhuniyo ca ārādhikā, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā brahmacārino ārādhakā, upāsakā ca gihī odātavasanā kāmabhogino ārādhakā, upāsikā ca gihiniyo odātavasanā brahmacāriniyo ārādhikā; But since Master Gotama and the bhikkhus and the bhikkhunīs and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, living the life divine, and the lay followers, householders clothed in white, enjoying sensual pleasures, and the female lay followers, housewives clothed in white, living the life divine, are accomplished in this Dhamma,

“āyasmā, bhante, vacchagotto bhagavato pāde sirasā vandati, evañca vadeti: “Venerable sir, the venerable Vacchagotta pays homage with his head at the Blessed One’s feet, and he says:“Sir, the mendicant Vacchagotta bows with his head to your feet and says:

‘pariciṇṇo me bhagavā, pariciṇṇo me sugato’”ti. ‘the Blessed One has been served by me, the Fortunate One has been served by me.’”‘I have served the Blessed One! I have served the Holy One!’”

“Pubbeva me, bhikkhave, vacchagotto bhikkhu cetasā ceto paricca vidito: “Bhikkhus, I already knew the bhikkhu Vacchagotta by encompassing his mind with my own mind, thus:“I’ve already encompassed Vacchagotta’s mind and understood that

‘tevijjo vacchagotto bhikkhu mahiddhiko mahānubhāvo’ti. ‘the bhikkhu Vacchagotta has the three true knowledges; he has great supernormal power and great might.’he has the three knowledges, and is very mighty and powerful.

Devatāpi me etamatthaṁ ārocesuṁ: And deities also told me of this matter:And deities also told me about this.”

‘tevijjo, bhante, vacchagotto bhikkhu mahiddhiko mahānubhāvo’”ti. ‘Venerable sir, the bhikkhu Vacchagotta has the three true knowledges; he has great supernormal power and great might.’”

Idamavoca bhagavā. This is what the Blessed One said.That is what the Buddha said.

Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti. Those bhikkhus were satisfied and delighted in the Blessed One’s words.Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

Mahāvacchasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ tatiyaṁ.
