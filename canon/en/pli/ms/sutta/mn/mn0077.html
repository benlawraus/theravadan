Majjhima Nikāya 77 Middle Discourses 77

Mahāsakuludāyisutta The Longer Discourse with Sakuludāyī

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—Thus I heard:So I have heard.

ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati veḷuvane kalandakanivāpe. On one occasion the Blessed One was living at Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the Squirrels’ Sanctuary.At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the squirrels’ feeding ground.

Tena kho pana samayena sambahulā abhiññātā abhiññātā paribbājakā moranivāpe paribbājakārāme paṭivasanti, seyyathidaṁ—Now on that occasion a number of very well-known wanderers were staying in the Wanderers’ Park, the Peacocks’ Sanctuary, that is to say—Now at that time several very well-known wanderers were residing in their monastery in the peacocks’ feeding ground.

annabhāro varadharo sakuludāyī ca paribbājako aññe ca abhiññātā abhiññātā paribbājakā. Annabhāra, Varadhara, and Sakuludāyī the Wanderer, as well as other very well-known wanderers.They included Annabhāra, Varadhara, Sakuludāyī, and other very well-known wanderers.

Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya rājagahaṁ piṇḍāya pāvisi. Then when it was morning, the Blessed One dressed, and taking his bowl and outer robe, he went into Rājagaha for alms.Then the Buddha robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, entered Rājagaha for alms.

Atha kho bhagavā yena moranivāpo paribbājakārāmo tenupasaṅkami. Then the Blessed One went to the Wanderers’ Park, the Peacocks’ Sanctuary.So the Buddha went to the monastery for the wanderers.

Tena kho pana samayena sakuludāyī paribbājako mahatiyā paribbājakaparisāya saddhiṁ nisinno hoti unnādiniyā uccāsaddamahāsaddāya anekavihitaṁ tiracchānakathaṁ kathentiyā, seyyathidaṁ—Now on that occasion Sakuludāyī the Wanderer was sitting together with a large assembly of wanderers who were shouting and making a great uproar and noise, talking many kinds of pointless talk, such as:Now at that time, Sakuludāyī was sitting together with a large assembly of wanderers making an uproar, a colossal racket. Addasā kho sakuludāyī paribbājako bhagavantaṁ dūratova āgacchantaṁ. Sakuludāyī the Wanderer saw the Blessed One coming in the distance.Sakuludāyī saw the Buddha coming off in the distance,

idamāsanaṁ paññattan”ti. this seat is ready.”

“Tiṭṭhatesā, bhante, kathā yāya mayaṁ etarahi kathāya sannisinnā. “Let that be, venerable sir, this talk for which we are now gathered here.“Sir, leave aside what we were sitting talking about just now.

Nesā, bhante, kathā bhagavato dullabhā bhavissati pacchāpi savanāya. It will not be hard for the Blessed One to hear about it later too, venerable sir.It won’t be hard for you to hear about that later.

Purimāni, bhante, divasāni purimatarāni nānātitthiyānaṁ samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṁ kutūhalasālāyaṁ sannisinnānaṁ sannipatitānaṁ ayamantarākathā udapādi: A few days ago, venerable sir, when ascetics and brahmins of various sects were gathered together, sitting in the debating hall, this discussion arose:Sir, a few days ago several ascetics and brahmins who follow various other religions were sitting together at the debating hall, and this discussion came up among them:

‘lābhā vata, bho, aṅgamagadhānaṁ, suladdhalābhā vata, bho, aṅgamagadhānaṁ. ‘It is a gain indeed for the people of Aṅga and Magadha, it is a great gain indeed for the people of Aṅga and Magadha,‘The people of Aṅga and Magadha are so fortunate, so very fortunate!

Tatrime samaṇabrāhmaṇā saṅghino gaṇino gaṇācariyā ñātā yasassino titthakarā sādhusammatā bahujanassa rājagahaṁ vassāvāsaṁ osaṭā. that these ascetics and brahmins, heads of orders, heads of groups, teachers of groups, well known and famous ford-makers,[^1] regarded as good by many people, have entered upon the rains residence at Rājagaha.For there are these ascetics and brahmins who lead an order and a community, and tutor a community. sopi rājagahaṁ vassāvāsaṁ osaṭo. he too has entered upon the rains residence at Rājagaha.

Ayampi kho makkhali gosālo …pe… There is also this Makkhali Gosāla …the bamboo-staffed ascetic Gosāla,

ajito kesakambalo … Ajita Kesakambalī …Ajita of the hair blanket,

Ko nu kho imesaṁ bhavataṁ samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṁ saṅghīnaṁ gaṇīnaṁ gaṇācariyānaṁ ñātānaṁ yasassinaṁ titthakarānaṁ sādhusammatānaṁ bahujanassa sāvakānaṁ sakkato garukato mānito pūjito, kañca pana sāvakā sakkatvā garuṁ katvā upanissāya viharantī’ti? Now, of these good ascetics and brahmins, heads of orders, heads of groups, teachers of groups, well known and famous ford-makers, regarded as good by many people, which one is honoured, respected, revered, and venerated by his disciples, and in dependence on whom, honouring and respecting him, do his disciples live?’Which of these ascetics and brahmins is honored, respected, revered, and venerated by their disciples? Tatrekacce evamāhaṁsu: Thereupon some said thus:Some of them said:

Tatra ca pana me sāvakā bahū abhiññāvosānapāramippattā viharanti. And, practising this, many of my disciples dwell having reached the consummation and perfection of direct knowledge.And many of my disciples meditate on that having attained perfection and consummation of insight.

Puna caparaṁ, udāyi, akkhātā mayā sāvakānaṁ paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipannā me sāvakā dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena satte passanti cavamāne upapajjamāne hīne paṇīte suvaṇṇe dubbaṇṇe sugate duggate yathākammūpage satte pajānanti: ‘ime vata bhonto sattā kāyaduccaritena samannāgatā vacīduccaritena samannāgatā manoduccaritena samannāgatā ariyānaṁ upavādakā micchādiṭṭhikā micchādiṭṭhikammasamādānā, te kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapannā; ime vā pana bhonto sattā kāyasucaritena samannāgatā vacīsucaritena samannāgatā manosucaritena samannāgatā ariyānaṁ anupavādakā sammādiṭṭhikā sammādiṭṭhikammasamādānā, te kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapannā’ti. Iti dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena satte passanti cavamāne upapajjamāne hīne paṇīte suvaṇṇe dubbaṇṇe sugate duggate yathākammūpage satte pajānanti. Again, Udāyī, I have proclaimed to my disciples the way, practising which my disciples, with the divine eye, purified and surpassing the human, see beings passing away and reappearing, inferior and superior, fair and ugly, fortunate and unfortunate, and understand how beings pass on according to their actions: ‘these beings who were ill-conducted in body, speech, and mind, revilers of the noble ones, wrong in view, undertaking action based on wrong view, on the dissolution of the body, after death, have reappeared in a state of deprivation, in a bad destination, in perdition, even in hell; but these beings who were well-conducted in body, speech, and mind, not revilers of the noble ones, right in view, undertaking action based on right view, on the dissolution of the body, after death, have reappeared in a good destination, even in the heavenly world’—thus, with the divine eye, purified and surpassing the human, they see beings passing away and reappearing, inferior and superior, fair and ugly, fortunate and unfortunate, and understand how beings pass on according to their actions.Furthermore, I have explained to my disciples a practice that they use so that, with clairvoyance that is purified and superhuman, they see sentient beings passing away and being reborn—inferior and superior, beautiful and ugly, in a good place or a bad place. They understand how sentient beings pass on according to their deeds: ‘These dear beings did bad things by way of body, speech, and mind. They denounced the noble ones; they had wrong view; and they chose to act out of that wrong view. When their body broke up, after death, they were reborn in a place of loss, a bad place, the underworld, hell. These dear beings, however, did good things by way of body, speech, and mind. They never denounced the noble ones; they had right view; and they chose to act out of that right view. When their body broke up, after death, they were reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.’ And so, with clairvoyance that is purified and superhuman, they see sentient beings passing away and being reborn—inferior and superior, beautiful and ugly, in a good place or a bad place. They understand how sentient beings pass on according to their deeds.

Seyyathāpi, udāyi, dve agārā sadvārā. Tatra cakkhumā puriso majjhe ṭhito passeyya manusse gehaṁ pavisantepi nikkhamantepi anucaṅkamantepi anuvicarantepi; Just as, Udāyī, there might be two houses with doors, and a man with good eyes standing between them might see people entering a house and leaving it, walking to and fro;Suppose there were two houses with doors. A person with clear eyes standing in between them would see humans entering and leaving a house and wandering to and fro.

evameva kho, udāyi, akkhātā mayā sāvakānaṁ paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipannā me sāvakā dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena satte passanti cavamāne upapajjamāne hīne paṇīte suvaṇṇe dubbaṇṇe sugate duggate yathākammūpage satte pajānanti …pe… so too, Udāyī, I have proclaimed to my disciples the way, practising which my disciples, with the divine eye, purified and surpassing the human, see beings passing away and reappearing … and understand how beings pass on according to their actions …In the same way, I have explained to my disciples a practice that they use so that, with clairvoyance that is purified and superhuman, they see sentient beings passing away and being reborn …

tatra ca pana me sāvakā bahū abhiññāvosānapāramippattā viharanti. And, practising this, many of my disciples dwell having reached the consummation and perfection of direct knowledge.And many of my disciples meditate on that having attained perfection and consummation of insight.

Puna caparaṁ, udāyi, akkhātā mayā sāvakānaṁ paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipannā me sāvakā āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharanti. Again, Udāyī, I have proclaimed to my disciples the way, practising which my disciples, by realizing for themselves with direct knowledge, here and now enter upon and abide in the deliverance of mind and the deliverance by wisdom that are taintless with the exhaustion of the taints.Furthermore, I have explained to my disciples a practice that they use to realize the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom. And they live having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements.

Seyyathāpi, udāyi, pabbatasaṅkhepe udakarahado accho vippasanno anāvilo, tattha cakkhumā puriso tīre ṭhito passeyya sippisambukampi sakkharakaṭhalampi macchagumbampi carantampi tiṭṭhantampi. Tassa evamassa: ‘ayaṁ kho udakarahado accho vippasanno anāvilo, tatrime sippisambukāpi sakkharakaṭhalāpi macchagumbāpi carantipi tiṭṭhantipī’ti. Just as, Udāyī, there might be a lake of water in a mountain recess, clear, limpid, and undisturbed, and a man with good eyes, standing on the bank, might see shells, gravel, and pebbles, and also shoals of fish swimming about and resting; he would think thus: ‘This lake of water is clear, limpid, and undisturbed, and there are these shells, gravel, and pebbles, and also these shoals of fish swimming about and resting’;Suppose that in a mountain glen there was a lake that was transparent, clear, and unclouded. A person with clear eyes standing on the bank would see the clams and mussels, pebbles and gravel, and schools of fish swimming about or staying still. They’d think: ‘This lake is transparent, clear, and unclouded. And here are the clams and mussels, pebbles and gravel, and schools of fish swimming about or staying still.’

Evameva kho, udāyi, akkhātā mayā sāvakānaṁ paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipannā me sāvakā āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharanti. so too, Udāyī, I have proclaimed to my disciples the way, practising which my disciples, by realizing for themselves with direct knowledge, here and now enter upon and abide in the deliverance of mind and the deliverance by wisdom that are taintless with the exhaustion of the taints.In the same way, I have explained to my disciples a practice that they use to realize the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom. And they live having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements.

Tatra ca pana me sāvakā bahū abhiññāvosānapāramippattā viharanti. And, practising this, many of my disciples dwell having reached the consummation and perfection of direct knowledge.And many of my disciples meditate on that having attained perfection and consummation of insight.

Ayaṁ kho, udāyi, pañcamo dhammo yena mama sāvakā sakkaronti garuṁ karonti mānenti pūjenti, sakkatvā garuṁ katvā upanissāya viharanti. This, Udāyī, is the fifth thing on account of which my disciples honour, respect, revere, and venerate me, and live in dependence on me, honouring and respecting me.This is the fifth quality because of which my disciples are loyal to me.

Ime kho, udāyi, pañca dhammā yehi mamaṁ sāvakā sakkaronti garuṁ karonti mānenti pūjenti, sakkatvā garuṁ katvā upanissāya viharantī”ti. These, Udāyī, are the five things on account of which my disciples honour, respect, revere, and venerate me, and live in dependence on me, honouring and respecting me.”These are the five qualities because of which my disciples honor, respect, revere, and venerate me; and honoring and respecting me, they remain loyal to me.”

Idamavoca bhagavā. This is what the Blessed One said.That is what the Buddha said.

Attamano sakuludāyī paribbājako bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandīti. Sakuludāyī the Wanderer was satisfied and delighted in the Blessed One’s words.Satisfied, the wanderer Sakuludāyī approved what the Buddha said.
