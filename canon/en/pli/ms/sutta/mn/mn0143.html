Majjhima Nikāya 143 Middle Discourses 143

Anāthapiṇḍikovādasutta Advice to Anāthapiṇḍika

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—Thus I heard:So I have heard.

ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. On one occasion the Blessed One was living at Sāvatthī, in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s Park.At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

Tena kho pana samayena anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati ābādhiko hoti dukkhito bāḷhagilāno. Now on that occasion Anāthapiṇḍika the householder was afflicted, suffering, and gravely ill.Now at that time the householder Anāthapiṇḍika was sick, suffering, gravely ill.

Atha kho anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati aññataraṁ purisaṁ āmantesi: Then Anāthapiṇḍika the householder addressed a certain man thus:Then he addressed a man,

“ehi tvaṁ, ambho purisa, yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkama; upasaṅkamitvā mama vacanena bhagavato pāde sirasā vandāhi: ‘Come, good man, go to the Blessed One, and in my name pay homage with your head at his feet, and say:“Please, my man, go to the Buddha, and in my name bow with your head to his feet. Say to him:

‘anāthapiṇḍiko, bhante, gahapati ābādhiko dukkhito bāḷhagilāno. “Venerable sir, Anāthapiṇḍika the householder is afflicted, suffering, and gravely ill.‘Sir, the householder Anāthapiṇḍika is sick, suffering, gravely ill.

So bhagavato pāde sirasā vandatī’ti. He pays homage with his head at the Blessed One’s feet.”He bows with his head to your feet.’

Yena cāyasmā sāriputto tenupasaṅkama; upasaṅkamitvā mama vacanena āyasmato sāriputtassa pāde sirasā vandāhi: Then go to the venerable Sāriputta, and in my name pay homage with your head at the venerable Sāriputta’s feet, and say:Then go to Venerable Sāriputta, and in my name bow with your head to his feet. Say to him:

‘anāthapiṇḍiko, bhante, gahapati ābādhiko dukkhito bāḷhagilāno. “Venerable sir, Anāthapiṇḍika the householder is afflicted, suffering, and gravely ill.‘Sir, the householder Anāthapiṇḍika is sick, suffering, gravely ill.

So āyasmato sāriputtassa pāde sirasā vandatī’ti. He pays homage with his head at the venerable Sāriputta’s feet.”He bows with his head to your feet.’

Evañca vadehi: And say further:And then say:

‘sādhu kira, bhante, āyasmā sāriputto yena anāthapiṇḍikassa gahapatissa nivesanaṁ tenupasaṅkamatu anukampaṁ upādāyā’”ti. “It would be good, venerable sir, if the venerable Sāriputta would come to the house of Anāthapiṇḍika the householder, out of compassion.”’‘Sir, please visit him at his home out of sympathy.’”

“Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho so puriso anāthapiṇḍikassa gahapatissa paṭissutvā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho so puriso bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: ‘Yes, venerable sir,’ that man replied to Anāthapiṇḍika the householder, and he went to the Blessed One, and after paying homage to him, he sat down at one side. Sitting at one side, that man said to the Blessed One:“Yes, sir,” that man replied. He did as Anāthapiṇḍika asked.

“anāthapiṇḍiko, bhante, gahapati ābādhiko dukkhito bāḷhagilāno. ‘Venerable sir, Anāthapiṇḍika the householder is afflicted, suffering, and gravely ill.

So bhagavato pāde sirasā vandatī”ti. He pays homage with his head at the Blessed One’s feet.’

Just as if a strong man were splitting my head open with a sharp sword, so too, venerable Sāriputta, violent winds cut through my head.The winds piercing my head are so severe, it feels like a strong man drilling into my head with a sharp point.

Just as if a strong man were tightening a tough leather strap round my head as a headband, so too, venerable Sāriputta, there are violent pains in my head.The pain in my head is so severe, it feels like a strong man tightening a strong leather strap around my head.
