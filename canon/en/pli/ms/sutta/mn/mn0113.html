Majjhima Nikāya 113 Middle Discourses 113Middle Discourses 113

Sappurisasutta A Person of IntegrityA True Person

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—I have heard thatSo I have heard.

ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. on one occasion the Blessed One was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi: There he addressed the monks:There the Buddha addressed the mendicants,

“bhikkhavo”ti. “Monks!”“Mendicants!”

“Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. “Yes, lord,” the monks responded to him.“Venerable sir,” they replied.

Bhagavā etadavoca: The Blessed One said,The Buddha said this:

“sappurisadhammañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi asappurisadhammañca. “Monks, I will teach you the quality of a person of integrity and the quality of a person of no integrity.“Mendicants, I will teach you the qualities of a true person and the qualities of an untrue person.

Taṁ suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha, bhāsissāmī”ti. Listen, and pay close attention. I will speak.”Listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

“Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. “Yes, lord,” the monks responded to him.“Yes, sir,” they replied.

Bhagavā etadavoca: The Blessed One said:The Buddha said this:

“Katamo ca, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo? “And which is the quality of a person of no integrity?“And what is a quality of an untrue person?

Idha, bhikkhave, asappuriso uccākulā pabbajito hoti. There is the case where a person of no integrity goes forth from a high-ranking family.Take an untrue person who has gone forth from an eminent family.

So iti paṭisañcikkhati: He notices,They reflect:

‘ahaṁ khomhi uccākulā pabbajito, ime panaññe bhikkhū na uccākulā pabbajitā’ti. ‘I have gone forth from a high-ranking family, but these other monks have not gone forth from a high-ranking family.’‘I have gone forth from an eminent family, unlike these other mendicants.’

So tāya uccākulīnatāya attānukkaṁseti, paraṁ vambheti. He exalts himself for having a high-ranking family and disparages others.And they glorify themselves and put others down on account of that.

Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo. This is the quality of a person of no integrity.This is a quality of an untrue person.

Sappuriso ca kho, bhikkhave, iti paṭisañcikkhati: But a person of integrity notices,A true person reflects:

‘na kho uccākulīnatāya lobhadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, dosadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, mohadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti. ‘It’s not through having a high-ranking family that the quality of greed goes to its end; it’s not through having a high-ranking family that the quality of aversion… the quality of delusion goes to its end.‘It’s not because of one’s eminent family that thoughts of greed, hate, or delusion come to an end.

No cepi uccākulā pabbajito hoti; Even though one has not gone forth from a high-ranking family,Even if someone has not gone forth from an eminent family,

so ca hoti dhammānudhammappaṭipanno sāmīcippaṭipanno anudhammacārī, so tattha pujjo, so tattha pāsaṁso’ti. if—practicing the Dhamma in line with the Dhamma, practicing masterfully—he is one who follows the Dhamma, he is to be honored for that, praised for that.’if they practice in line with the teaching, practice properly, and live in line with the teaching, they are worthy of honor and praise for that.’

So paṭipadaṁyeva antaraṁ karitvā tāya uccākulīnatāya nevattānukkaṁseti na paraṁ vambheti. So, giving priority just to the practice, he neither exalts himself for having a high-ranking family nor disparages others.Keeping the practice to themselves, they don’t glorify themselves and put others down on account of their eminent family.

Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo. This is the quality of a person of integrity.This is a quality of a true person.

Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, asappuriso mahākulā pabbajito hoti …pe… And further, a person of no integrity goes forth from a great family…Furthermore, take an untrue person who has gone forth from a great family …

mahābhogakulā pabbajito hoti …pe… a family of great wealth…from a wealthy family …

uḷārabhogakulā pabbajito hoti. a family of extensive wealth.from an exceptionally wealthy family.

So iti paṭisañcikkhati: He notices,They reflect:

Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, asappuriso lābhī hoti cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārānaṁ. And further, a person of no integrity is one who gains robe-cloth, alms-food, lodgings, & medicinal requisites for the sick.Furthermore, take an untrue person who receives robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick.

ekāsaniko hoti. one who eats only one meal a day.who eats in one sitting per day.

So iti paṭisañcikkhati: He notices,They reflect:

‘ahaṁ khomhi ekāsaniko, ime panaññe bhikkhū na ekāsanikā’ti. ‘I am one who eats only one meal a day, but these other monks do not eat only one meal a day.’‘I eat in one sitting per day, unlike these other mendicants.’

So tena ekāsanikattena attānukkaṁseti, paraṁ vambheti. He exalts himself for being one who eats only one meal a day and disparages others.And they glorify themselves and put others down on account of that.

Ayampi, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo. This is the quality of a person of no integrity.This too is a quality of an untrue person.

Sappuriso ca kho, bhikkhave, iti paṭisañcikkhati: But a person of integrity notices,A true person reflects:

‘na kho ekāsanikattena lobhadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, dosadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, mohadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti. ‘It’s not through being one who eats only one meal a day that the quality of greed goes to its end; it’s not through being one who eats only one meal a day that the quality of aversion… the quality of delusion goes to its end.‘It’s not because of eating in one sitting per day that thoughts of greed, hate, or delusion come to an end.

No cepi ekāsaniko hoti; Even though one is not one who eats only one meal a day,Even if someone eats in more than one sitting per day,

so ca hoti dhammānudhammappaṭipanno sāmīcippaṭipanno anudhammacārī, so tattha pujjo, so tattha pāsaṁso’ti. if—practicing the Dhamma in line with the Dhamma, practicing masterfully—he is one who follows the Dhamma, he is to be honored for that, praised for that.’if they practice in line with the teaching, practice properly, and live in line with the teaching, they are worthy of honor and praise for that.’

So paṭipadaṁyeva antaraṁ karitvā tena ekāsanikattena nevattānukkaṁseti, na paraṁ vambheti. So, giving priority just to the practice, he neither exalts himself for being one who eats only one meal a day nor disparages others.Keeping the practice to themselves, they don’t glorify themselves and put others down on account of their eating in one sitting per day.

Ayampi, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo. This is the quality of a person of integrity.This too is a quality of a true person.

Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, sappuriso sabbaso nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma saññāvedayitanirodhaṁ upasampajja viharati. Paññāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā honti. Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu na kiñci maññati, na kuhiñci maññati, na kenaci maññatī”ti.
