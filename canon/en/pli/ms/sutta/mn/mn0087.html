“Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja, vāsabhāya te khattiyāya vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā uppajjeyyuṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? ‘How do you conceive this, sire, if change and alteration took place in the noble Queen Vāsabbā (would that bring) sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair?’

“Vāsabhāya me, mallike, khattiyāya vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā jīvitassapi siyā aññathattaṁ, kiṁ pana me na uppajjissanti sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? ‘A change and alteration in the noble Queen Vāsabbā would mean an alteration in my life. How could sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair not arise in me?’

“Idaṁ kho taṁ, mahārāja, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena sandhāya bhāsitaṁ: ‘It was with reference to that, sire, that the Blessed One who knows and sees, Arahant and Fully Enlightened, said:

‘piyajātikā sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā piyappabhavikā’ti. “Dear ones, those who are dear, (bring) sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair.”’

Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja, piyo te viṭaṭūbho senāpatī”ti? ‘How do you conceive this, sire, is general Viḍūḍabha dear to you ?’Do you love your son, General Viḍūḍabha? …

“Evaṁ, mallike, piyo me viṭaṭūbho senāpatī”ti. ‘Yes, Mallikā, general Viḍūḍabha is dear to me.’

“Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja, viṭaṭūbhassa te senāpatissa vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā uppajjeyyuṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? ‘How do you conceive this, sire, if change and alteration took place in general Viḍūḍabha (would that bring) sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair?’

“Viṭaṭūbhassa me, mallike, senāpatissa vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā jīvitassapi siyā aññathattaṁ, kiṁ pana me na uppajjissanti sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? ‘A change and alteration in general Viḍūḍabha would mean an alteration in my life. How could sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair not arise in me?’

“Idaṁ kho taṁ, mahārāja, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena sandhāya bhāsitaṁ: ‘It was with reference to that, sire, that the Blessed One who knows and sees, Arahant and Fully Enlightened, said:

“Evaṁ, mallike, piyā mesi tvan”ti. ‘Yes, Mallikā, you are dear to me.’“Indeed I do love you, Mallikā.”

“Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja, ‘How do you conceive this, sire, “What do you think, Great King?

mayhaṁ te vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā uppajjeyyuṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? if change and alteration took place in me (would that bring) sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair?’If I were to decay and perish, would sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress arise in you?”

“Tuyhañhi me, mallike, vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā jīvitassapi siyā aññathattaṁ, kiṁ pana me na uppajjissanti sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? ‘A change and alteration in you would mean an alteration in my life. How could sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair not arise in me?’“If you were to decay and perish, my life would fall apart. How could sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress not arise in me?”

“Idaṁ kho taṁ, mahārāja, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena sandhāya bhāsitaṁ: ‘It was with reference to that, sire, that the Blessed One who knows and sees, Arahant and Fully Enlightened, said: “This is what the Buddha was referring to when he said:

‘piyajātikā sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā piyappabhavikā’ti. “Dear ones, those who are dear, (bring) sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair.”’‘Our loved ones are a source of sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress.’

Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja, ‘How do you conceive this, sire, What do you think, Great King?

piyā te kāsikosalā”ti? are Kāsi and Kosala dear to you ?’Do you love the realms of Kāsi and Kosala?”

“Evaṁ, mallike, piyā me kāsikosalā. ‘Yes Mallikā, Kāsi and Kosala are dear to me. “Indeed I do, Mallikā.

“Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja, ‘How do you conceive this, sire, “What do you think, Great King?

kāsikosalānaṁ te vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā uppajjeyyuṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā”ti? if change and alteration took place in Kāsi and Kosala, (would that bring) sorrow and lamentation, pain, grief and despair?’If these realms were to decay and perish, would sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress arise in you?”
