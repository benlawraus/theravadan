addhā, bhikkhave, tathāgato pubbe sukatakammakārī yaṁ etarahi evarūpā anāsavā sukhā vedanā vedeti. then surely, bhikkhus, the Tathāgata was a doer of good deeds in the past, since he now feels such taintless, pleasant feelings.clearly the Realized One has done good deeds in the past, since he now experiences such undefiled pleasure.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā issaranimmānahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of creation by a supreme god,If sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of God Almighty’s creation,

addhā, bhikkhave, tathāgato bhaddakena issarena nimmito yaṁ etarahi evarūpā anāsavā sukhā vedanā vedeti. then surely, bhikkhus, the Tathāgata was created by a good supreme god, since he now feels such taintless, pleasant feelings.clearly the Realized One was created by a good God, since he now experiences such undefiled pleasure.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā saṅgatibhāvahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of circumstance,If sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of circumstance and nature,

addhā, bhikkhave, tathāgato kalyāṇasaṅgatiko yaṁ etarahi evarūpā anāsavā sukhā vedanā vedeti. then surely, bhikkhus, the Tathāgata has good circumstances, since he now feels such taintless, pleasant feelings.clearly the Realized One arises from good circumstances, since he now experiences such undefiled pleasure.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā abhijātihetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of class of origin,If sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of the class of rebirth,

addhā, bhikkhave, tathāgato kalyāṇābhijātiko yaṁ etarahi evarūpā anāsavā sukhā vedanā vedeti. then surely, bhikkhus, the Tathāgata has a good class of origin, since he now feels such taintless, pleasant feelings.clearly the Realized One was reborn in a good class, since he now experiences such undefiled pleasure.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā diṭṭhadhammūpakkamahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of present exertion,If sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of exertion in this life,

addhā, bhikkhave, tathāgato kalyāṇadiṭṭhadhammūpakkamo yaṁ etarahi evarūpā anāsavā sukhā vedanā vedeti. then surely, bhikkhus, the Tathāgata has made good present exertion, since he now feels such taintless, pleasant feelings.clearly the Realized One exerts himself well in this life, since he now experiences such undefiled pleasure.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā pubbekatahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of what was done in the past, the Tathāgata is to be praised;The Realized One deserves praise whether or not sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of past deeds,

no ce sattā pubbekatahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato. if beings do not experience pleasure and pain because of what was done in the past, the Tathāgata is to be praised.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā issaranimmānahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of creation by a supreme god, the Tathāgata is to be praised;or God Almighty’s creation,

no ce sattā issaranimmānahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato. if beings do not experience pleasure and pain because of creation by a supreme god, the Tathāgata is to be praised.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā saṅgatibhāvahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of circumstance, the Tathāgata is to be praised;or circumstance and nature,

no ce sattā saṅgatibhāvahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato. if beings do not experience pleasure and pain because of circumstance, the Tathāgata is to be praised.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā abhijātihetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of class of origin, the Tathāgata is to be praised;or class of rebirth,

no ce sattā abhijātihetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato. if beings do not experience pleasure and pain because of class of origin, the Tathāgata is to be praised.

Sace, bhikkhave, sattā diṭṭhadhammūpakkamahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato; If, bhikkhus, beings experience pleasure and pain because of present exertion, the Tathāgata is to be praised;

no ce sattā diṭṭhadhammūpakkamahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti, pāsaṁso tathāgato. if beings do not experience pleasure and pain because of present exertion, the Tathāgata is to be praised.or exertion in this life.

Evaṁvādī, bhikkhave, tathāgatā. This, bhikkhus, is the doctrine of the Tathāgatas.Such is the doctrine of the Realized One.

Evaṁvādīnaṁ, bhikkhave, tathāgatānaṁ ime dasa sahadhammikā pāsaṁsaṭṭhānā āgacchantī”ti. For the Tathāgatas who hold this doctrine, bhikkhus, these ten legitimate grounds for praise apply.”Saying this, the Realized One deserves praise on these ten legitimate grounds.”

Idamavoca bhagavā. This is what the Blessed One said.That is what the Buddha said.

Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti. Those bhikkhus were satisfied and delighted in the Blessed One’s words.Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

Devadahasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
