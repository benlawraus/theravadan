Sandakasutta With Sandaka

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—Thus I heard:So I have heard.

Tena kho pana samayena sandako paribbājako pilakkhaguhāyaṁ paṭivasati mahatiyā paribbājakaparisāya saddhiṁ pañcamattehi paribbājakasatehi. Atha kho āyasmā ānando sāyanhasamayaṁ paṭisallānā vuṭṭhito bhikkhū āmantesi: Then when it was evening, the venerable Ānanda rose from meditation and addressed the bhikkhus thus:Then in the late afternoon, Venerable Ānanda came out of retreat and addressed the mendicants:

“Evaṁ, bho”ti kho sandako paribbājako āyasmato ānandassa paccassosi. “Yes, sir,” Sandaka the Wanderer replied to the venerable Ānanda.“Yes, worthy sir,” replied Sandaka.

Āyasmā ānando etadavoca: The venerable Ānanda said this:Venerable Ānanda said this:

“cattārome, sandaka, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena abrahmacariyavāsā akkhātā cattāri ca anassāsikāni brahmacariyāni akkhātāni, yattha viññū puriso sasakkaṁ brahmacariyaṁ na vaseyya, vasanto ca nārādheyya ñāyaṁ dhammaṁ kusalan”ti. “Sandaka, these four kinds of life that are not the life divine have been declared by that Blessed One who knows and sees, the Arahant, the Fully Enlightened One, and these four kinds of life divine without consolation have also been declared, wherein a wise man would certainly not live the life divine, or, if he did live it, he would not succeed in the true method, the wholesome Dhamma.”“Sandaka, these things have been explained by the Blessed One, who knows and sees, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha: four ways that negate the spiritual life, and four kinds of unreliable spiritual life. A sensible person would, to the best of their ability, not practice such spiritual paths, and if they did practice them, they wouldn’t succeed in the system of the skillful teaching.”

“Katame pana te, bho ānanda, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena cattāro abrahmacariyavāsā akkhātā, yattha viññū puriso sasakkaṁ brahmacariyaṁ na vaseyya, vasanto ca nārādheyya ñāyaṁ dhammaṁ kusalan”ti? “But, Master Ānanda, what are those four kinds of life that are not the life divine, declared by that Blessed One who knows and sees, the Arahant, the Fully Enlightened One, wherein a wise man would certainly not live the life divine, or, if he did live it, he would not succeed in the true method, the wholesome Dhamma?”“But worthy Ānanda, what are the four ways that negate the spiritual life, and the four kinds of unreliable spiritual life?”

“Idha, sandaka, ekacco satthā evaṁvādī hoti evaṁdiṭṭhi: “Here, Sandaka, a certain teacher has this doctrine and this view:“Sandaka, take a certain teacher who has this doctrine and view:

‘natthi dinnaṁ, natthi yiṭṭhaṁ, natthi hutaṁ, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṁ kammānaṁ phalaṁ vipāko, natthi ayaṁ loko, natthi paroloko, natthi mātā, natthi pitā, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi loke samaṇabrāhmaṇā sammaggatā sammāpaṭipannā ye imañca lokaṁ parañca lokaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedenti. ‘There is nothing given, nothing offered, nothing sacrificed; there is no fruit or result of good and bad actions; there is no this world, no other world; there is no mother, no father; there are no beings spontaneously reborn; there are no ascetics or brahmins in the world who have themselves realized by direct knowledge and declared this world and the other world, who have travelled the right path and practised rightly.[^2]‘There’s no meaning in giving, sacrifice, or offerings. There’s no fruit or result of good and bad deeds. There’s no afterlife. There’s no such thing as mother and father, or beings that are reborn spontaneously. And there’s no ascetic or brahmin who is rightly comported and rightly practiced, and who describes the afterlife after realizing it with their own insight.

Cātumahābhūtiko ayaṁ puriso yadā kālaṁ karoti, pathavī pathavīkāyaṁ anupeti anupagacchati, āpo āpokāyaṁ anupeti anupagacchati, tejo tejokāyaṁ anupeti anupagacchati, vāyo vāyokāyaṁ anupeti anupagacchati, ākāsaṁ indriyāni saṅkamanti. The fire in their body progresses and proceeds to the substance of fire. The air in their body progresses and proceeds to the substance of air. The faculties are transferred to space.

Yasmiṁ kho, sandaka, satthari sāvako evarūpaṁ uḷāravisesaṁ adhigacchati tattha viññū puriso sasakkaṁ brahmacariyaṁ vaseyya, vasanto ca ārādheyya ñāyaṁ dhammaṁ kusalan”ti. “Yo pana so, bho ānanda, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññā vimutto paribhuñjeyya so kāme”ti? “But, Master Ānanda, would a bhikkhu who is an Arahant with the taints exhausted, who has lived the life, done what was to be done, laid down the burden, reached his own goal, destroyed the fetters of being, and is completely liberated through final knowledge—would he indulge in sensual pleasures?”“But worthy Ānanda, when a mendicant is perfected—with defilements ended, who has completed the spiritual journey, done what had to be done, laid down the burden, achieved their heart’s goal, utterly ended the fetter of continued existence, and is rightly freed through enlightenment—could they still enjoy sensual pleasures?”

“Yo so, sandaka, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto, abhabbo so pañcaṭṭhānāni ajjhācarituṁ. “Sandaka, a bhikkhu who is an Arahant with the taints exhausted, who has lived the life, done what was to be done, laid down the burden, reached his own goal, destroyed the fetters of being, and is completely liberated through final knowledge, is incapable of transgressing in five instances.“Sandaka, a mendicant who is perfected—with defilements ended, who has completed the spiritual journey, done what had to be done, laid down the burden, achieved their heart’s goal, utterly ended the fetter of continued existence, and is rightly freed through enlightenment—can’t transgress in five respects.

Abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu sañcicca pāṇaṁ jīvitā voropetuṁ, abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu adinnaṁ theyyasaṅkhātaṁ ādātuṁ, abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu methunaṁ dhammaṁ paṭisevetuṁ, abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu sampajānamusā bhāsituṁ, abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu sannidhikārakaṁ kāme paribhuñjituṁ, seyyathāpi pubbe agāriyabhūto. Yo so, sandaka, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto, abhabbo so imāni pañcaṭṭhānāni ajjhācaritun”ti. A bhikkhu who is an Arahant with the taints exhausted, who has lived the life, done what was to be done, laid down the burden, reached his own goal, destroyed the fetters of being, and is completely liberated through final knowledge, is incapable of transgressing in these five instances.”A mendicant who is perfected can’t transgress in these five respects.”

“Yo pana so, bho ānanda, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto tassa carato ceva tiṭṭhato ca suttassa ca jāgarassa ca satataṁ samitaṁ ñāṇadassanaṁ paccupaṭṭhitaṁ: “But, Master Ānanda, for a bhikkhu who is an Arahant with the taints exhausted, who has lived the life, done what was to be done, laid down the burden, reached his own goal, destroyed the fetters of being, and is completely liberated through final knowledge, is the knowledge and vision continuously and unceasingly present to him, whether he is walking or standing, asleep or awake, that:“But worthy Ānanda, when a mendicant is perfected, would the knowledge and vision that their defilements are ended be constantly and continually present to them, while walking, standing, sleeping, and waking?”

“Kīvabahukā pana, bho ānanda, imasmiṁ dhammavinaye niyyātāro”ti? “But, Master Ānanda, how many guides[^7] are there in this Dhamma and Discipline?”“But Reverend Ānanda, in this teaching and training, how many are emancipated?”
