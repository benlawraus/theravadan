Majjhima Nikāya 145 Middle Discourses 145

Puṇṇovādasutta Advice to Puṇṇa

Evaṁ me sutaṁ—Thus I heard:So I have heard.

ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. On one occasion the Blessed One was living at Sāvatthī, in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s Park.At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

Atha kho āyasmā puṇṇo sāyanhasamayaṁ paṭisallānā vuṭṭhito yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā puṇṇo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: Then the venerable Puṇṇa, rising from meditation in the evening, went to the Blessed One, and after paying homage to him, he sat down at one side. Sitting at one side, the venerable Puṇṇa said to the Blessed One:Then in the late afternoon, Venerable Puṇṇa came out of retreat and went to the Buddha. He bowed, sat down to one side, and said to the Buddha,

“sādhu maṁ, bhante, bhagavā saṅkhittena ovādena ovadatu, yamahaṁ bhagavato dhammaṁ sutvā eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto vihareyyan”ti. ‘Venerable sir, it would be good if the Blessed One would instruct me in brief, so that, having heard the Dhamma from the Blessed One, I might dwell alone, withdrawn, diligent, ardent, and resolute.’“Sir, may the Buddha please teach me Dhamma in brief. When I’ve heard it, I’ll live alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute.”

“Tena hi, puṇṇa, suṇāhi, sādhukaṁ manasi karohi; bhāsissāmī”ti. ‘Then, Puṇṇa, listen and heed well what I shall say.’“Well then, Puṇṇa, listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

“Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā puṇṇo bhagavato paccassosi. ‘Yes, venerable sir,’ the venerable Puṇṇa replied.“Yes, sir,” replied Puṇṇa.

Bhagavā etadavoca: The Blessed One said this:The Buddha said this:

“Santi kho, puṇṇa, cakkhuviññeyyā rūpā iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṁhitā rajanīyā. ‘Puṇṇa, there are forms cognizable by the eye that are wished for, desired, agreeable and likeable, connected with sensual desire and provocative of lust.“Puṇṇa, there are sights known by the eye, which are likable, desirable, agreeable, pleasant, sensual, and arousing.

Tañce bhikkhu abhinandati abhivadati ajjhosāya tiṭṭhati. If a bhikkhu relishes, welcomes, and remains holding to that,If a mendicant approves, welcomes, and keeps clinging to them,

Tassa taṁ abhinandato abhivadato ajjhosāya tiṭṭhato uppajjati nandī. then, as he relishes, welcomes, and remains holding to it, relishing arises in him.this gives rise to relishing.

‘Nandīsamudayā dukkhasamudayo, puṇṇā’ti vadāmi. With the arising of relishing, Puṇṇa, there is the arising of suffering, I say.Relishing is the origin of suffering, I say.

Santi kho, puṇṇa, sotaviññeyyā saddā … There are sounds cognizable by the ear …There are sounds known by the ear …

ghānaviññeyyā gandhā … odours cognizable by the nose …smells known by the nose …

jivhāviññeyyā rasā … flavours cognizable by the tongue …tastes known by the tongue …

kāyaviññeyyā phoṭṭhabbā … tangibles cognizable by the body …touches known by the body …

manoviññeyyā dhammā iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṁhitā rajanīyā. dhammas cognizable by the mind that are wished for, desired, agreeable and likeable, connected with sensual desire and provocative of lust.ideas known by the mind, which are likable, desirable, agreeable, pleasant, sensual, and arousing.

Tañce bhikkhu abhinandati abhivadati ajjhosāya tiṭṭhati. If a bhikkhu relishes, welcomes, and remains holding to that,If a mendicant approves, welcomes, and keeps clinging to them,

Tassa taṁ abhinandato abhivadato ajjhosāya tiṭṭhato uppajjati nandī. then, as he relishes, welcomes, and remains holding to it, relishing arises in him.this gives rise to relishing.

‘Nandīsamudayā dukkhasamudayo, puṇṇā’ti vadāmi. With the arising of relishing, Puṇṇa, there is the arising of suffering, I say.Relishing is the origin of suffering, I say.

Santi ca kho, puṇṇa, cakkhuviññeyyā rūpā iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṁhitā rajanīyā. But, Puṇṇa, there are forms cognizable by the eye that are wished for, desired, agreeable and likeable, connected with sensual desire and provocative of lust.There are sights known by the eye, which are likable, desirable, agreeable, pleasant, sensual, and arousing.

Tañce bhikkhu nābhinandati nābhivadati nājjhosāya tiṭṭhati. If a bhikkhu does not relish, welcome, or remain holding to that,If a mendicant doesn’t approve, welcome, and keep clinging to them,

Tassa taṁ anabhinandato anabhivadato anajjhosāya tiṭṭhato nandī nirujjhati. then, as he does not relish, welcome, or remain holding to it, relishing ceases in him.relishing ceases.

‘Nandīnirodhā dukkhanirodho, puṇṇā’ti vadāmi. With the ceasing of relishing, Puṇṇa, there is the ceasing of suffering, I say.When relishing ceases, suffering ceases, I say.

Santi ca kho, puṇṇa, sotaviññeyyā saddā … There are sounds cognizable by the ear …There are sounds known by the ear …

ghānaviññeyyā gandhā … odours cognizable by the nose …smells known by the nose …

jivhāviññeyyā rasā … flavours cognizable by the tongue …tastes known by the tongue …

kāyaviññeyyā phoṭṭhabbā … tangibles cognizable by the body …touches known by the body …

manoviññeyyā dhammā iṭṭhā kantā manāpā piyarūpā kāmūpasaṁhitā rajanīyā. dhammas cognizable by the mind that are wished for, desired, agreeable and likeable, connected with sensual desire and provocative of lust.ideas known by the mind, which are likable, desirable, agreeable, pleasant, sensual, and arousing.

Tañce bhikkhu nābhinandati nābhivadati nājjhosāya tiṭṭhati. If a bhikkhu does not relish, welcome, or remain holding to that,If a mendicant doesn’t approve, welcome, and keep clinging to them,

Tassa taṁ anabhinandato anabhivadato anajjhosāya tiṭṭhato nandī nirujjhati. then, as he does not relish, welcome, or remain holding to it, relishing ceases in him.relishing ceases.

‘Nandīnirodhā dukkhanirodho, puṇṇā’ti vadāmi. With the ceasing of relishing, Puṇṇa, there is the ceasing of suffering, I say.When relishing ceases, suffering ceases, I say.

Iminā ca tvaṁ, puṇṇa, mayā saṅkhittena ovādena ovadito katarasmiṁ janapade viharissasī”ti? ‘Now, Puṇṇa, having been given this brief instruction by me, in which country will you dwell?’Puṇṇa, now that I’ve given you this brief advice, what country will you live in?”

“Imināhaṁ, bhante, bhagavatā saṅkhittena ovādena ovadito, atthi sunāparanto nāma janapado, tatthāhaṁ viharissāmī”ti. ‘Venerable sir, having been given this brief instruction by the Blessed One, there is a country called Sunāparanta; I will dwell there.’“Sir, there’s a country named Sunāparanta. I shall live there.”

“Caṇḍā kho, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā; ‘Fierce, Puṇṇa, are the people of Sunāparanta;“The people of Sunāparanta are wild and rough, Puṇṇa.

pharusā kho, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā. rough, Puṇṇa, are the people of Sunāparanta.

Sace taṁ, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā akkosissanti paribhāsissanti, tattha te, puṇṇa, kinti bhavissatī”ti? If the people of Sunāparanta abuse and threaten you, Puṇṇa, what will you think then?’If they abuse and insult you, what will you think of them?”

“Sace maṁ, bhante, sunāparantakā manussā akkosissanti paribhāsissanti, tattha me evaṁ bhavissati: ‘Venerable sir, if the people of Sunāparanta abuse and threaten me, then I shall think:“If they abuse and insult me, I will think:

‘bhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, subhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, yaṁ me nayime pāṇinā pahāraṁ dentī’ti. “These people of Sunāparanta are good indeed, truly good indeed, in that they do not give me a blow with the fist.”‘These people of Sunāparanta are gracious, truly gracious, since they don’t hit me with their fists.’

Evamettha, bhagavā, bhavissati; That, Blessed One, is what I shall think;That’s what I’ll think, Blessed One.

evamettha, sugata, bhavissatī”ti. that, Sublime One, is what I shall think.’That’s what I’ll think, Holy One.”

“Sace pana te, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā pāṇinā pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha pana te, puṇṇa, kinti bhavissatī”ti? ‘But, Puṇṇa, if the people of Sunāparanta give you a blow with the fist, what will you think then?’“But if they do hit you with their fists, what will you think of them then?”

“Sace me, bhante, sunāparantakā manussā pāṇinā pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha me evaṁ bhavissati: ‘Venerable sir, if the people of Sunāparanta give me a blow with the fist, then I shall think:“If they hit me with their fists, I’ll think:

‘bhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, subhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, yaṁ me nayime leḍḍunā pahāraṁ dentī’ti. “These people of Sunāparanta are good indeed, truly good indeed, in that they do not give me a blow with a clod.”‘These people of Sunāparanta are gracious, truly gracious, since they don’t throw stones at me.’

Evamettha, bhagavā, bhavissati; That, Blessed One, is what I shall think;That’s what I’ll think, Blessed One.

evamettha, sugata, bhavissatī”ti. that, Sublime One, is what I shall think.’That’s what I’ll think, Holy One.”

“Sace pana te, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā leḍḍunā pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha pana te, puṇṇa, kinti bhavissatī”ti? ‘But, Puṇṇa, if the people of Sunāparanta give you a blow with a clod, what will you think then?’“But if they do throw stones at you, what will you think of them then?”

“Sace me, bhante, sunāparantakā manussā leḍḍunā pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha me evaṁ bhavissati: ‘Venerable sir, if the people of Sunāparanta give me a blow with a clod, then I shall think:“If they throw stones at me, I’ll think:

‘bhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, subhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, yaṁ me nayime daṇḍena pahāraṁ dentī’ti. “These people of Sunāparanta are good indeed, truly good indeed, in that they do not give me a blow with a stick.”‘These people of Sunāparanta are gracious, truly gracious, since they don’t beat me with a club.’

Evamettha, bhagavā, bhavissati; That, Blessed One, is what I shall think;That’s what I’ll think, Blessed One.

evamettha, sugata, bhavissatī”ti. that, Sublime One, is what I shall think.’That’s what I’ll think, Holy One.”

“Sace pana te, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā daṇḍena pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha pana te, puṇṇa, kinti bhavissatī”ti? ‘But, Puṇṇa, if the people of Sunāparanta give you a blow with a stick, what will you think then?’“But if they do beat you with a club, what will you think of them then?”

“Sace me, bhante, sunāparantakā manussā daṇḍena pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha me evaṁ bhavissati: ‘Venerable sir, if the people of Sunāparanta give me a blow with a stick, then I shall think:“If they beat me with a club, I’ll think:

‘bhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, subhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, yaṁ me nayime satthena pahāraṁ dentī’ti. “These people of Sunāparanta are good indeed, truly good indeed, in that they do not give me a blow with a knife.”‘These people of Sunāparanta are gracious, truly gracious, since they don’t stab me with a knife.’

Evamettha, bhagavā, bhavissati; That, Blessed One, is what I shall think;That’s what I’ll think, Blessed One.

evamettha, sugata, bhavissatī”ti. that, Sublime One, is what I shall think.’That’s what I’ll think, Holy One.”

“Sace pana te, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā satthena pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha pana te, puṇṇa, kinti bhavissatī”ti? ‘But, Puṇṇa, if the people of Sunāparanta give you a blow with a knife, what will you think then?’“But if they do stab you with a knife, what will you think of them then?”

“Sace me, bhante, sunāparantakā manussā satthena pahāraṁ dassanti, tattha me evaṁ bhavissati: ‘Venerable sir, if the people of Sunāparanta give me a blow with a knife, then I shall think:“If they stab me with a knife, I’ll think:

‘bhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, subhaddakā vatime sunāparantakā manussā, yaṁ maṁ nayime tiṇhena satthena jīvitā voropentī’ti. “These people of Sunāparanta are good indeed, truly good indeed, in that they do not deprive me of life with a sharp knife.”‘These people of Sunāparanta are gracious, truly gracious, since they don’t take my life with a sharp knife.’

Evamettha, bhagavā, bhavissati; That, Blessed One, is what I shall think;That’s what I’ll think, Blessed One.

evamettha, sugata, bhavissatī”ti. that, Sublime One, is what I shall think.’That’s what I’ll think, Holy One.”

“Sace pana taṁ, puṇṇa, sunāparantakā manussā tiṇhena satthena jīvitā voropessanti, tattha pana te, puṇṇa, kinti bhavissatī”ti? ‘But, Puṇṇa, if the people of Sunāparanta deprive you of life with a sharp knife, what will you think then?’“But if they do take your life with a sharp knife, what will you think of them then?”

“Sace maṁ, bhante, sunāparantakā manussā tiṇhena satthena jīvitā voropessanti, tattha me evaṁ bhavissati: ‘Venerable sir, if the people of Sunāparanta deprive me of life with a sharp knife, then I shall think:“If they take my life with a sharp knife, I’ll think:

‘santi kho bhagavato sāvakā kāye ca jīvite ca aṭṭīyamānā harāyamānā jigucchamānā satthahārakaṁ pariyesanti. Taṁ me idaṁ apariyiṭṭhaṁyeva satthahārakaṁ laddhan’ti. “There have been disciples of the Blessed One who, humiliated and disgusted by the body and by life, sought for a knife-bringer. I have found this knife-bringer without seeking him.”‘There are disciples of the Buddha who looked for a suicide weapon because they were horrified, repelled, and disgusted with the body and with life. And I have found this without looking!’

Evamettha, bhagavā, bhavissati; That, Blessed One, is what I shall think;That’s what I’ll think, Blessed One.

evamettha, sugata, bhavissatī”ti. that, Sublime One, is what I shall think.’That’s what I’ll think, Holy One.”

“Sādhu sādhu, puṇṇa. ‘Good, good, Puṇṇa.“Good, good Puṇṇa!

Sakkhissasi kho tvaṁ, puṇṇa, iminā damūpasamena samannāgato sunāparantasmiṁ janapade viharituṁ. Possessing such self-control and peacefulness, you will be able to dwell in the country of Sunāparanta.Having such self-control and peacefulness, you will be quite capable of living in Sunāparanta.

Yassadāni tvaṁ, puṇṇa, kālaṁ maññasī”ti. Now, Puṇṇa, you may go at your own convenience.’Now, Puṇṇa, go at your convenience.”

Atha kho āyasmā puṇṇo bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinanditvā anumoditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā senāsanaṁ saṁsāmetvā pattacīvaramādāya yena sunāparanto janapado tena cārikaṁ pakkāmi. Then the venerable Puṇṇa, having delighted in and rejoiced in the Blessed One’s words, rose from his seat, and after paying homage to the Blessed One, keeping him on his right, he set his lodging in order, and taking his bowl and outer robe, set out on tour toward the country of Sunāparanta.And then Puṇṇa welcomed and agreed with the Buddha’s words. He got up from his seat, bowed and respectfully circled the Buddha, keeping him on his right. Then he set his lodgings in order and, taking his bowl and robe, set out for Sunāparanta.

Anupubbena cārikaṁ caramāno yena sunāparanto janapado tadavasari. Wandering on tour by stages, he arrived at the country of Sunāparanta.Traveling stage by stage, he arrived at Sunāparanta,

Tatra sudaṁ āyasmā puṇṇo sunāparantasmiṁ janapade viharati. There the venerable Puṇṇa dwelt in the country of Sunāparanta.and stayed there.

Atha kho āyasmā puṇṇo tenevantaravassena pañcamattāni upāsakasatāni paṭivedesi, tenevantaravassena pañcamattāni upāsikasatāni paṭivedesi, tenevantaravassena tisso vijjā sacchākāsi. Then, within that same rainy season, the venerable Puṇṇa established about five hundred male lay followers and about five hundred female lay followers, and within that same rainy season he himself realized the three true knowledges.Within that rainy season he confirmed around five hundred male and five hundred female lay followers. And within that same rainy season he realized the three knowledges.

Atha kho āyasmā puṇṇo aparena samayena parinibbāyi. Then, after some time, the venerable Puṇṇa attained final Nibbāna.Some time later he became fully extinguished.

Atha kho sambahulā bhikkhū yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdiṁsu. Ekamantaṁ nisinnā kho te bhikkhū bhagavantaṁ etadavocuṁ: Then a number of bhikkhus went to the Blessed One, and after paying homage to him, they sat down at one side. Sitting at one side, those bhikkhus said to the Blessed One:Then several mendicants went up to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, and said to him,

“yo so, bhante, puṇṇo nāma kulaputto bhagavatā saṅkhittena ovādena ovadito so kālaṅkato. ‘Venerable sir, the clansman named Puṇṇa, to whom the Blessed One gave brief instruction, has died.“Sir, the gentleman named Puṇṇa, who was advised in brief by the Buddha, has passed away.

Tassa kā gati, ko abhisamparāyo”ti? What is his destination, what is his future course?’Where has he been reborn in his next life?”

“Paṇḍito, bhikkhave, puṇṇo kulaputto paccapādi dhammassānudhammaṁ, na ca maṁ dhammādhikaraṇaṁ viheṭhesi. ‘Bhikkhus, the clansman Puṇṇa was wise; he practised in accordance with the Dhamma, and he did not trouble me over the interpretation of the Dhamma.“Mendicants, Puṇṇa was astute. He practiced in line with the teaching, and did not trouble me about the teachings.

Parinibbuto, bhikkhave, puṇṇo kulaputto”ti. The clansman Puṇṇa, bhikkhus, has attained final Nibbāna.’Puṇṇa has become fully quenched.”

Idamavoca bhagavā. This is what the Blessed One said.That is what the Buddha said.

Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti. Those bhikkhus were satisfied and delighted in the Blessed One’s words.Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

Puṇṇovādasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ tatiyaṁ. The Discourse Giving Advice to Puṇṇa, the third, is finished.
