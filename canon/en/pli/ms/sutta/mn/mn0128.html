‘ko nu kho hetu ko paccayo yvāhaṁ obhāsañhi kho sañjānāmi na ca rūpāni passāmi; “What is the reason, what is the condition, why I perceive illumination and do not see forms,‘What is the cause, what is the reason for this?’

rūpāni hi kho passāmi na ca obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi—and why I see forms and do not perceive illumination,

kevalampi rattiṁ, kevalampi divaṁ, kevalampi rattindivan’ti. for a whole night, for a whole day, for a whole night and day?”

Tassa mayhaṁ, anuruddhā, etadahosi: I thought thus, Anuruddha:It occurred to me:

‘yasmiñhi kho ahaṁ samaye rūpanimittaṁ amanasikaritvā obhāsanimittaṁ manasi karomi, obhāsañhi kho tasmiṁ samaye sañjānāmi, na ca rūpāni passāmi. “On the occasion on which I give attention to the sign of forms without giving attention to the sign of illumination, on that occasion I perceive illumination and do not see forms.‘When I don’t focus on the basis of the forms, but focus on the basis of the light, then I perceive light and do not see forms.

Yasmiṁ panāhaṁ samaye obhāsanimittaṁ amanasikaritvā rūpanimittaṁ manasi karomi, rūpāni hi kho tasmiṁ samaye passāmi na ca obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi—But on the occasion on which I give attention to the sign of illumination without giving attention to the sign of forms, on that occasion I see forms and do not perceive illumination—But when I don’t focus on the basis of the light, but focus on the basis of the forms, then I see forms and do not perceive light.

kevalampi rattiṁ, kevalampi divaṁ, kevalampi rattindivan’ti. for a whole night, for a whole day, for a whole night and day.”And this goes on for a whole night, a whole day, even a whole night and day.’

So kho ahaṁ, anuruddhā, appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto parittañceva obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi, parittāni ca rūpāni passāmi; ‘As I dwelt, Anuruddha, diligent, ardent, and self-controlled, I perceived limited illumination and saw limited forms;While meditating diligent, keen, and resolute, I perceived limited light and saw limited forms,

appamāṇañceva obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi, appamāṇāni ca rūpāni passāmi: I perceived measureless illumination and saw measureless forms—or I perceived limitless light and saw limitless forms.

‘kevalampi rattiṁ, kevalampi divaṁ, kevalampi rattindivaṁ’. for a whole night, for a whole day, for a whole night and day.And this went on for a whole night, a whole day, even a whole night and day.

Tassa mayhaṁ, anuruddhā, etadahosi: I thought thus, Anuruddha:I thought:

‘ko nu kho hetu ko paccayo yvāhaṁ parittañceva obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi, parittāni ca rūpāni passāmi; “What is the reason, what is the condition, why I perceive limited illumination and see limited forms,‘What is the cause, what is the reason for this?’

appamāṇañceva obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi, appamāṇāni ca rūpāni passāmi—and why I perceive measureless illumination and see measureless forms—

kevalampi rattiṁ, kevalampi divaṁ, kevalampi rattindivan’ti. for a whole night, for a whole day, for a whole night and day?”

Tassa mayhaṁ, anuruddhā, etadahosi: I thought thus, Anuruddha:It occurred to me:

‘yasmiṁ kho me samaye paritto samādhi hoti, parittaṁ me tasmiṁ samaye cakkhu hoti. “On the occasion on which my concentration is limited, on that occasion my eye is limited.‘When my immersion is limited, then my vision is limited,

Sohaṁ parittena cakkhunā parittañceva obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi, parittāni ca rūpāni passāmi. With a limited eye I perceive limited illumination and see limited forms.and with limited vision I perceive limited light and see limited forms.

Yasmiṁ pana me samaye appamāṇo samādhi hoti, appamāṇaṁ me tasmiṁ samaye cakkhu hoti. But on the occasion on which my concentration is measureless, on that occasion my eye is measureless.But when my immersion is limitless, then my vision is limitless,

Sohaṁ appamāṇena cakkhunā appamāṇañceva obhāsaṁ sañjānāmi, appamāṇāni ca rūpāni passāmi—With a measureless eye I perceive measureless illumination and see measureless forms—and with limitless vision I perceive limitless light and see limitless forms.

kevalampi rattiṁ, kevalampi divaṁ, kevalampi rattindivan’ti. for a whole night, for a whole day, for a whole night and day.”And this goes on for a whole night, a whole day, even a whole night and day.’

Yato kho me, anuruddhā, ‘vicikicchā cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā vicikicchā cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, ‘As soon as I knew, Anuruddha, “Uncertainty is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.After understanding that doubt,

‘amanasikāro cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā amanasikāro cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Inattention is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.loss of focus,

‘thinamiddhaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā thinamiddhaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Lethargy and drowsiness is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.dullness and drowsiness,

‘chambhitattaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā chambhitattaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Alarm is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.terror,

‘uppilaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā uppilaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Elation is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.excitement,

‘duṭṭhullaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā duṭṭhullaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Inertia is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.discomfort,

‘accāraddhavīriyaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā accāraddhavīriyaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Too active an energy is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.excessive energy,

‘atilīnavīriyaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā atilīnavīriyaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Too sluggish an energy is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.overly lax energy,

‘abhijappā cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā abhijappā cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Longing is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.longing,

‘nānattasaññā cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā nānattasaññā cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi, As soon as I knew, “Perception of diversity is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.perception of diversity,

‘atinijjhāyitattaṁ rūpānaṁ cittassa upakkileso’ti—iti viditvā atinijjhāyitattaṁ rūpānaṁ cittassa upakkileso pahīno ahosi. As soon as I knew, “Too much meditating upon forms is an imperfection of mind,” it was abandoned in me.and excessive concentration on forms are corruptions of the mind, I had given them up.

Tassa mayhaṁ, anuruddhā, etadahosi: ‘I thought thus, Anuruddha:I thought:

‘ye kho me cittassa upakkilesā te me pahīnā. “The imperfections of my mind have been abandoned by me.‘I’ve given up my mental corruptions.

Handa dānāhaṁ tividhena samādhiṁ bhāvemī’ti. Now let me develop concentration in three ways.”Now let me develop immersion in three ways.’

So kho ahaṁ, anuruddhā, savitakkampi savicāraṁ samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ, avitakkampi vicāramattaṁ samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ, avitakkampi avicāraṁ samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ, sappītikampi samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ, nippītikampi samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ, sātasahagatampi samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ, upekkhāsahagatampi samādhiṁ bhāvesiṁ. So, Anuruddha, I developed concentration with initial application and sustained application; I developed concentration without initial application and with sustained application only; I developed concentration without initial application and without sustained application; I developed concentration accompanied by happiness; I developed concentration without happiness; I developed concentration accompanied by pleasure; I developed concentration accompanied by equanimity.I developed immersion while placing the mind and keeping it connected; without placing the mind, merely keeping it connected; without placing the mind or keeping it connected; with rapture; without rapture; with pleasure; with equanimity.

Yato kho me, anuruddhā, savitakkopi savicāro samādhi bhāvito ahosi, avitakkopi vicāramatto samādhi bhāvito ahosi, avitakkopi avicāro samādhi bhāvito ahosi, sappītikopi samādhi bhāvito ahosi, nippītikopi samādhi bhāvito ahosi, sātasahagatopi samādhi bhāvito ahosi, upekkhāsahagatopi samādhi bhāvito ahosi. ‘When, Anuruddha, concentration with initial application and sustained application had been developed by me, concentration without initial application and with sustained application only had been developed by me, concentration without initial application and without sustained application had been developed by me, concentration accompanied by happiness had been developed by me, concentration without happiness had been developed by me, concentration accompanied by pleasure had been developed by me, concentration accompanied by equanimity had been developed by me,When I had developed immersion in these ways,

Ñāṇañca pana me dassanaṁ udapādi, then knowledge and vision arose in me:the knowledge and vision arose in me:

akuppā me cetovimutti. Ayamantimā jāti, natthi dāni punabbhavo”ti. “My deliverance is unshakeable; this is the last birth; there is now no more renewal of being.”’‘My freedom is unshakable; this is my last rebirth; now there’ll be no more future lives.’”

Idamavoca bhagavā. This is what the Blessed One said.That is what the Buddha said.

Attamano āyasmā anuruddho bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandīti. The venerable Anuruddha was satisfied and delighted in the Blessed One’s words.Satisfied, Venerable Anuruddha approved what the Buddha said.
