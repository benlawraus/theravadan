Theragāthā 1.56 Verses of the Senior Monks 1.56

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Chaṭṭhavagga Chapter Six

Kuṭivihārittheragāthā Kuṭivihārin (1st)

“Ko kuṭikāyaṁ bhikkhu kuṭikāyaṁ, “Who is in this hut?” “A monk is in this hut,

Vītarāgo susamāhitacitto; free of lust, his mind serene.

Evaṁ jānāhi āvuso, Reverend, you should know this:

Amoghā te kuṭikā katā”ti. your hut wasn’t built in vain.”

… Kuṭivihāritthero ….
