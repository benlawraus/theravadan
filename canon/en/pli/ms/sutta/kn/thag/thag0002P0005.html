Theragāthā 2.5 Verses of the Senior Monks 2.5

Dukanipāta The Book of the Twos

Paṭhamavagga Chapter One

Ajinattheragāthā Ajina

“Api ce hoti tevijjo, Even a master of the three knowledges,

maccuhāyī anāsavo; undefiled, conqueror of death,

Appaññātoti naṁ bālā, is looked down upon for being unknown

avajānanti ajānatā. by ignorant fools.

Yo ca kho annapānassa, But any individual here

lābhī hotidha puggalo; who gets food and drink

Pāpadhammopi ce hoti, is honored by them,

so nesaṁ hoti sakkato”ti. even if they are of bad character.

… Ajino thero ….
