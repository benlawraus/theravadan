Cariyāpiṭaka The Conduct Leading to Buddhahood

Yudhañjayavagga The Chapter With Yudhañjaya

Saccapāramī 2 The Perfection of Truth (2nd)

Saccatāpasacariya The Ascetic Truthful’s Conduct

“Punāparaṁ yadā homi, “Then again when I was

Tāpaso saccasavhayo; an ascetic known as Truthful,

Saccena lokaṁ pālesiṁ, I protected the world with my truth,

Samaggaṁ janamakāsahan”ti. uniting the people.”

Saccatāpasacariyaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
