Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 42

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Atthakāmavagga Wanting to Help

2. Kapotajātaka The Pigeon: a past life story

“Yo atthakāmassa hitānukampino, One who does not follow advice when instructed

Ovajjamāno na karoti sāsanaṁ; by an ally who wants to help,

Kapotakassa vacanaṁ akatvā, not following the pigeon’s counsel,

Amittahatthatthagatova setī”ti. ends up in the hands of their foes.

Kapotajātakaṁ dutiyaṁ.
