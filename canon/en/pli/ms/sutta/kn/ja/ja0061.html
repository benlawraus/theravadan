Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 61

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Itthivagga Women

1. Asātamantajātaka The Spell of the Unpleasant: a past life story

“Asā lokitthiyo nāma, The women in the world called unclaimed—

velā tāsaṁ na vijjati; there is no confining them.

Sārattā ca pagabbhā ca, They are rude and full of desires,

sikhī sabbaghaso yathā; like an all-consuming flame.

Tā hitvā pabbajissāmi, Leaving them behind, I shall go forth

vivekamanubrūhayan”ti. and foster seclusion.

Asātamantajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
