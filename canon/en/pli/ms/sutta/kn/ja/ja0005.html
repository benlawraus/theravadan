5. Taṇḍulanāḷijātaka A Cup of Rice: a past life story

“Kimagghati taṇḍulanāḷikāyaṁ, “Speak, O King: what value has a cup of rice

Assāna mūlāya vadehi rāja; for the pricing of horses?”

Bārāṇasiṁ santarabāhirantaṁ, “He values a cup of rice

Ayamagghati taṇḍulanāḷikā”ti. at Varanasi with its suburbs!”

Taṇḍulanāḷijātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.
