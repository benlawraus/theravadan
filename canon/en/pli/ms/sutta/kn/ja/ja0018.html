Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 18

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Sīlavagga Ethics

8. Matakabhattajātaka The Feast for the Dead: a past life story

“Evañce sattā jāneyyuṁ, If beings only knew

dukkhāyaṁ jātisambhavo; how this suffering is created by birth,

Na pāṇo pāṇinaṁ haññe, no creature would kill another,

pāṇaghātī hi socatī”ti. for the slayer of creatures grieves.

Matakabhattajātakaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
