Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 1

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Apaṇṇakavagga Sure Bet

1. Apaṇṇakajātaka Sure Bet: a past life story

Namo tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. Homage to him, the blessed one, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha!

“Apaṇṇakaṁ ṭhānameke, Some stand on a sure bet,

Dutiyaṁ āhu takkikā; while sophists speak of a second way.

Etadaññāya medhāvī, An intelligent person, knowing this,

Taṁ gaṇhe yadapaṇṇakan”ti. would take up the sure bet.

Apaṇṇakajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
