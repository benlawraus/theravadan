Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 62

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Itthivagga Women

2. Aṇḍabhūtajātaka Since Infancy: a past life story

“Yaṁ brāhmaṇo avādesi, The brahmin who played

vīṇaṁ samukhaveṭhito; the harp while blindfolded

Aṇḍabhūtā bhatā bhariyā, had raised his wife since infancy—

tāsu ko jātu vissase”ti. who would ever trust such women?

Aṇḍabhūtajātakaṁ dutiyaṁ.
