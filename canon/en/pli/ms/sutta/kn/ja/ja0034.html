Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 34

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Kulāvakavagga Nests

4. Macchajātaka The Fish: a past life story

“Na maṁ sītaṁ na maṁ uṇhaṁ, It’s not the cold or heat for me,

na maṁ jālasmi bādhanaṁ; nor the being caught in the net;

Yañca maṁ maññate macchī, it’s that my lady, the fish, thinks of me,

aññaṁ so ratiyā gato”ti. “He went for joy to another.”

Macchajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
