Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 22

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Kuruṅgavagga The Four-horned Antelope

2. Kukkurajātaka Hounds: a past life story

“Ye kukkurā rājakulamhi vaddhā, The hounds raised in the royal family,

Koleyyakā vaṇṇabalūpapannā; pedigreed, handsome and strong,

Teme na vajjhā mayamasma vajjhā, are not slaughtered, we are slaughtered—

Nāyaṁ saghaccā dubbalaghātikāyan”ti. this is not the dogs deserving death, this is the killing of the weak.

Kukkurajātakaṁ dutiyaṁ.
