Jātaka Stories of Past Lives 52

Ekakanipāta The Book of the Ones

Āsīsavagga Hope

2. Cūḷajanakajātaka A Short Tale of Janaka: a past life story

“Vāyametheva puriso, A man should make an effort

na nibbindeyya paṇḍito; the astute should not be disillusioned.

Passāmi vohaṁ attānaṁ, Now I truly see myself,

udakā thalamubbhatan”ti. lifted from the water to the shore.

Cūḷajanakajātakaṁ dutiyaṁ.
