Sutta Nipāta 2.1 Sutta Nipāta 2.1Anthology of Discourses 2.1

Ratanasutta TreasuresGems

Yānīdha bhūtāni samāgatāni, Whatever spirits have gathered here,Whatever beings have gathered here,

Bhummāni vā yāni va antalikkhe; —on the earth, in the sky—on the ground or in the sky:

Sabbeva bhūtā sumanā bhavantu, may you all be happymay beings all be of happy heart,

Athopi sakkacca suṇantu bhāsitaṁ. & listen intently to what I say.and listen carefully to what is said.

Tasmā hi bhūtā nisāmetha sabbe, Thus, spirits, you should all be attentive.So pay heed, all you beings,

Mettaṁ karotha mānusiyā pajāya; Show goodwill to the human race.have love for humankind,

Divā ca ratto ca haranti ye baliṁ, Day & night they bring offerings,who day and night bring offerings;

Tasmā hi ne rakkhatha appamattā. so, being heedful, protect them.please protect them diligently.

Yaṁ kiñci vittaṁ idha vā huraṁ vā, Whatever wealth—here or beyond—There’s no wealth in this life or the next,

Saggesu vā yaṁ ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ; whatever exquisite treasure in the heavens,no sublime gem in the heavens,

Na no samaṁ atthi tathāgatena, does not, for us, equal the Tathāgata.that equals the Realized One.

Idampi buddhe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ; This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Buddha.This sublime gem is in the Buddha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Khayaṁ virāgaṁ amataṁ paṇītaṁ, The exquisite deathless—ending, dispassion—Ending, dispassion, the undying, the sublime,

Yadajjhagā sakyamunī samāhito; discovered by the Sakyan Sage in concentration:attained by the Sakyan Sage immersed in samādhi;

Na tena dhammena samatthi kiñci, There is nothing to equal that Dhamma.there is nothing equal to that Dhamma.

Idampi dhamme ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ; This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Dhamma.This sublime gem is in the Dhamma:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Yaṁ buddhaseṭṭho parivaṇṇayī suciṁ, What the excellent Awakened One extolled as pureThe purity praised by the highest Buddha

Samādhimānantarikaññamāhu; and called the concentration of unmediated knowing1:is said to be the “immersion with immediate fruit”;

Samādhinā tena samo na vijjati, No equal to that concentration can be found.no equal to that immersion is found.

Idampi dhamme ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ; This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Dhamma.This sublime gem is in the Dhamma:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Ye puggalā aṭṭha sataṁ pasatthā, The eight persons, praised by those at peace—The eight individual persons praised by the good

Cattāri etāni yugāni honti; these are the four pairs:are the four pairs of the Holy One’s disciples;

Te dakkhiṇeyyā sugatassa sāvakā, They, disciples of the One Well-Gone, deserve offerings.they are worthy of religious donations,

Etesu dinnāni mahapphalāni; What is given to them bears great fruit.what’s given to them is very fruitful.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Ye suppayuttā manasā daḷhena, Those who, devoted, firm-minded,Dedicated to Gotama’s dispensation,

Nikkāmino gotamasāsanamhi; apply themselves to Gotama’s message,strong-minded, free of sense desire,

Te pattipattā amataṁ vigayha, on attaining their goal, plunge into the deathless,they’ve attained the goal, plunged into freedom from death,

Laddhā mudhā nibbutiṁ bhuñjamānā; freely enjoying the liberation they’ve gained.and enjoy the quenching they’ve freely gained.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Yathindakhīlo pathavissito siyā, An Indra pillar,2 planted in the earth,As Indra’s pillar rooted in earth

Catubbhi vātehi asampakampiyo; that even the four winds cannot shake:is not shaken by the four winds,

Tathūpamaṁ sappurisaṁ vadāmi, That, I tell you, is like the person of integrity,I say a true person is like this,

Yo ariyasaccāni avecca passati; who—having comprehended the noble truths—sees.who sees the noble truths in experience.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Ye ariyasaccāni vibhāvayanti, Those who have seen clearly the noble truthsThose who fathom the noble truths

Gambhīrapaññena sudesitāni; well-taught by the one deeply discerning—taught by the one of deep wisdom,

Kiñcāpi te honti bhusaṁ pamattā, regardless of what [later] might make them heedless—do not take an eighth life,

Na te bhavaṁ aṭṭhamamādiyanti; will come to no eighth state of becoming,3even if they are hugely negligent.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Sahāvassa dassanasampadāya, At the moment of attaining sight,When they attain to vision

Tayassu dhammā jahitā bhavanti; one abandons three things:they give up three things:

Sakkāyadiṭṭhī vicikicchitañca, identity-views, uncertainty,substantialist view, doubt, and any

Sīlabbataṁ vāpi yadatthi kiñci. & any attachment to habits & practices.4attachment to precepts and observances.

Catūhapāyehi ca vippamutto, One is completely released from the four states of deprivation,5They’re freed from the four places of loss,

Chaccābhiṭhānāni abhabba kātuṁ; and incapable of committing the six great wrongs.6and cannot do six things.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Kiñcāpi so kamma karoti pāpakaṁ, Whatever bad deed one may doEven if they do a bad deed

Kāyena vācā uda cetasā vā; —in body, speech, or in mind—by body, speech, or mind,

Abhabba so tassa paṭicchadāya, one cannot hide it:they are unable to conceal it;

Abhabbatā diṭṭhapadassa vuttā; an incapability ascribed to one who has seen the Way.this, it’s said, is the inability of one who has seen the truth.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Vanappagumbe yatha phussitagge, Like a forest grove with flowering topsLike a tall forest tree crowned with flowers

Gimhānamāse paṭhamasmiṁ gimhe; in the first month of the heat of the summer,in the first month of summer;

Tathūpamaṁ dhammavaraṁ adesayi, so is the foremost Dhamma he taught,that’s how he taught the superb Dhamma,

Nibbānagāmiṁ paramaṁ hitāya; for the highest benefit, leading to unbinding.leading to extinguishment, the ultimate benefit.

Idampi buddhe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Buddha.This sublime gem is in the Buddha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Varo varaññū varado varāharo, Foremost, foremost-knowing, foremost-giving, foremost-bringing,The superb, knower of the superb, giver of the superb, bringer of the superb;

Anuttaro dhammavaraṁ adesayi; unsurpassed, he taught the foremost Dhamma.taught the superb Dhamma supreme.

Idampi buddhe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Buddha.This sublime gem is in the Buddha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Khīṇaṁ purāṇaṁ nava natthi sambhavaṁ, Ended the old, there is no new taking birth.The old is ended, nothing new is produced;

Virattacittāyatike bhavasmiṁ; Dispassioned their minds toward future becoming,their minds have no desire for future rebirth.

Te khīṇabījā avirūḷhichandā, they, with no seed, no desire for growth,Withered are the seeds, there’s no desire for growth,

Nibbanti dhīrā yathāyaṁ padīpo; enlightened, go out like this flame.7those attentive ones are extinguished like this lamp.

Idampi saṅghe ratanaṁ paṇītaṁ, This, too, is an exquisite treasure in the Saṅgha.This sublime gem is in the Saṅgha:

Etena saccena suvatthi hotu. By this truth may there be well-being.by this truth, may you be well!

Yānīdha bhūtāni samāgatāni, Whatever spirits have gathered here,Whatever beings have gathered here,

Bhummāni vā yāni va antalikkhe; —on the earth, in the sky—on the ground or in the sky:

Tathāgataṁ devamanussapūjitaṁ, the Tathāgata worshipped by beings human & divine—the Realized One is honored by gods and humans!

Buddhaṁ namassāma suvatthi hotu. let us pay homage to the Buddha. May there be well-being.We bow to the Buddha! May you be safe!

Yānīdha bhūtāni samāgatāni, Whatever spirits have gathered here,Whatever beings have gathered here,

Bhummāni vā yāni va antalikkhe; —on the earth, in the sky—on the ground or in the sky:

Tathāgataṁ devamanussapūjitaṁ, the Tathāgata worshipped by beings human & divine—the Realized One is honored by gods and humans!

Dhammaṁ namassāma suvatthi hotu. let us pay homage to the Dhamma. May there be well-being.We bow to the Dhamma! May you be safe!

Yānīdha bhūtāni samāgatāni, Whatever spirits have gathered here,Whatever beings have gathered here,

Bhummāni vā yāni va antalikkhe; —on the earth, in the sky—on the ground or in the sky:

Tathāgataṁ devamanussapūjitaṁ, the Tathāgata worshipped by beings human & divine—the Realized One is honored by gods and humans!

Saṅghaṁ namassāma suvatthi hotūti. let us pay homage to the Saṅgha. May there be well-being.We bow to the Saṅgha! May you be safe!
