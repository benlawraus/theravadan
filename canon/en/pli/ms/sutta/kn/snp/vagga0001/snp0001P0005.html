Sutta Nipāta 1.5 Sutta Nipāta 1.5Anthology of Discourses 1.5

Cundasutta CundaWith Cunda

“Pucchāmi muniṁ pahūtapaññaṁ, “I ask the sage of abundant discernment,“I ask the sage abounding in wisdom,”

(iti cundo kammāraputto) (Cunda the smith)said Cunda the smith,

Buddhaṁ dhammassāmiṁ vītataṇhaṁ; awakened, lord of the Dhamma, free of craving,“the Buddha, master of the teaching, free of craving,

Dvipaduttamaṁ sārathīnaṁ pavaraṁ, supreme among two-legged beings, best of charioteers:best of men, excellent charioteer, please tell me this:

Kati loke samaṇā tadiṅgha brūhi”. How many contemplatives are there in the world? Please tell me.”how many ascetics are there in the world?”

“Caturo samaṇā na pañcamatthi, “Four contemplatives, there is no fifth.“There are four ascetics, not a fifth.”

(cundāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)said the Buddha to Cunda,

Te te āvikaromi sakkhipuṭṭho; Being asked in person, I disclose them to you:“Being asked to bear witness, I will explain them to you:

Maggajino maggadesako ca, the path-victor, the path-explainer,the path-victor, the path-teacher,

Magge jīvati yo ca maggadūsī”. one who lives by the path, and the path-corrupter.”the path-liver, and the path-wrecker.”

“Kaṁ maggajinaṁ vadanti buddhā, “Whom do the awakened call the path-victor?“Who is a path-victor according to the Buddhas?”

(iti cundo kammāraputto) (Cunda)said Cunda the smith,

Maggakkhāyī kathaṁ atulyo hoti; How is one unequaled in pointing out the path?“and how is one an unequaled explainer of the path?

Magge jīvati me brūhi puṭṭho, When asked, tell me about the one who lives by the path.Tell me when asked about one who lives the path,

Atha me āvikarohi maggadūsiṁ”. Then disclose the path-corrupter to me.”then declare the path-wrecker.”

“Yo tiṇṇakathaṅkatho visallo, “Whoever, de-arrowed, has crossed over doubt,“Rid of indecision, free of thorns,

Nibbānābhirato anānugiddho; is refreshed in unbinding, devoid of greed,delighting in extinguishment, not fawning,

Lokassa sadevakassa netā, a guide to the world along with its devas:a guide for the world with its gods.

Tādiṁ maggajinaṁ vadanti buddhā. The awakened call such a person path-victor.The Buddhas say one such is victor of the path.

Paramaṁ paramanti yodha ñatvā, Whoever here knowing the foremost as foremost,Knowing the ultimate as ultimate,

Akkhāti vibhajate idheva dhammaṁ; who points out, who analyzes the Dhamma right here,they explain and analyze the teaching right here.

Taṁ kaṅkhachidaṁ muniṁ anejaṁ, he, the cutter of doubt, the sage unperturbed,That sage unstirred, with doubt cut off,

Dutiyaṁ bhikkhunamāhu maggadesiṁ. is called the second of monks: path-explainer.is the second mendicant, I say, the teacher of the path.

Yo dhammapade sudesite, Whoever, in the well-explained Dhamma-way,Living restrained and mindful on the path

Magge jīvati saññato satīmā; lives by the path, restrained, mindful,of the well-taught passage of teaching,

Anavajjapadāni sevamāno, partaking of blameless ways,cultivating blameless states,

Tatiyaṁ bhikkhunamāhu maggajīviṁ. is called the third of monks: with the path as his life.is the third mendicant, I say, the path-liver.

Chadanaṁ katvāna subbatānaṁ, Making a façade of good practices,Dressed like one true to their vows,

Pakkhandī kuladūsako pagabbho; brash, reckless, a corrupter of families,pushy, rude, a corrupter of families,

Māyāvī asaññato palāpo, idle, deceitful, unrestrained,devious, unrestrained, chaff,

Patirūpena caraṁ sa maggadūsī. & going about as a counterfeit: he is a path-corrupter.the path-wrecker’s life is a sham.

Ete ca paṭivijjhi yo gahaṭṭho, And when any householder understands these,A layperson who gets this,

Sutavā ariyasāvako sapañño; —an instructed disciple of the noble ones, discerning—a learned, wise noble disciple,

Sabbe netādisāti ñatvā, knowing, ‘Not all are like that one,’knows that ‘They are not all like that one’.

Iti disvā na hāpeti tassa saddhā; seeing one like that doesn’t kill his conviction.So when they see them they don’t lose their faith.

Kathaṁ hi duṭṭhena asampaduṭṭhaṁ, For how would one equate the corrupt with the uncorrupt,For how could one equate them—

Suddhaṁ asuddhena samaṁ kareyyā”ti. the impure with the pure?”the corrupt with the uncorrupt, the pure with the impure?”

Cundasuttaṁ pañcamaṁ. The Cunda Sutta, the fifth.
