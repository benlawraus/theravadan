Dhaniyasutta Discourse to Dhaniya Dhaniya the CattlemanThe Wealthy Rancher

“Pakkodano duddhakhīrohamasmi, “I have prepared cooked rice and fresh milk,” “The rice is cooked, my milking done.“I’ve boiled my rice and drawn my milk,”

(iti dhaniyo gopo) said Dhaniya the Cowherd, (Dhaniya the cattleman)said Dhaniya the rancher,

Anutīre mahiyā samānavāso; “I stay constantly at the shore of the great river, I live with my people along the banks of the Mahi;“I stay with my family along the bank of the Mahī.

Channā kuṭi āhito gini, “I have a closed hut with a fire kindled; my hut is roofed, my fire lit:My hut is roofed, my sacred fire kindled:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Akkodhano vigatakhilohamasmi, “I have no anger and no corruption,” “Free from anger, my rigidity gone,“I boil not with anger and have drawn out hard-heartedness,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Anutīre mahiyekarattivāso; “I stay one night at the shore of the great river, I live for one night along the banks of the Mahi;“I stay for one night along the bank of the Mahī.

Vivaṭā kuṭi nibbuto gini, “I have an open hut with the fire extinguished; my hut’s roof is open, my fire out.My hut is wide open, my fire is quenched:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Andhakamakasā na vijjare, “There are no gadflies or mosquitoes,” “No mosquitoes or gadflies are to be found.“No gadflies or mosquitoes are found,”

(iti dhaniyo gopo) said Dhaniya the Cowherd, (Dhaniya the cattleman)said Dhaniya,

Kacche rūḷhatiṇe caranti gāvo; “In the marshes and meadows where the cattle roam, The cows range in the marshy meadow where the grasses flourish.“cows graze on the lush meadow grass.

Vuṭṭhimpi saheyyumāgataṁ, “When it rained in the past, they could endure; They could stand the rain if it came:They get by even when the rain comes:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Baddhāsi bhisī susaṅkhatā, “The raft was assembled and well-contructed,” “A raft, well-made, has been lashed together.“I bound a raft and prepared it well,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Tiṇṇo pāragato vineyya oghaṁ; “It crossed over to the far shore, it went past the deluge; Having crossed over, gone to the far shore, I’ve subdued the flood.“and with it I crossed over, went to the far shore, and dispelled the flood.

Attho bhisiyā na vijjati, “I have no need of a raft; No need for a raft is to be found:Now I have no need for a raft:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Gopī mama assavā alolā, “My cowherd-woman listens and is prudent,” “My wife is composed, not wanton,“My wife is obedient, not wanton,”

(iti dhaniyo gopo) said Dhaniya the Cowherd, (Dhaniya the cattleman)said Dhaniya,

Dīgharattaṁ saṁvāsiyā manāpā; “For a long time, she has been a pleasant co-resident, is charming, has lived with me long.“long have we lived together happily.

Tassā na suṇāmi kiñci pāpaṁ, “I have never heard anything evil from her; I hear no evil about her at all:I hear nothing bad about her:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Cittaṁ mama assavaṁ vimuttaṁ, “My mind is unpolluted and liberated,” “My mind is composed, released,“My mind is obedient and freed,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Dīgharattaṁ paribhāvitaṁ sudantaṁ; “For a long time, it has been thoroughly developed and well-trained, has long been nurtured, well tamed.“long nurtured and well-tamed.

Pāpaṁ pana me na vijjati, “No evil can be found in me; No evil is to be found in me:Nothing bad is found in me:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Attavetanabhatohamasmi, “I have a servant that I hired myself,” “I support myself on my earnings.“I am self-employed,”

(iti dhaniyo gopo) said Dhaniya the Cowherd, (Dhaniya the cattleman)said Dhaniya,

Puttā ca me samāniyā arogā; “My children are all equally healthy, My sons live in harmony, free from disease.“and my healthy children likewise.

Tesaṁ na suṇāmi kiñci pāpaṁ, “I have never heard anything evil from them; I hear no evil about them at all:I hear nothing bad about them:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Nāhaṁ bhatakosmi kassaci, “I am not the servant of anyone,” “I’m in no one’s employ,“I am no one’s lackey,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Nibbiṭṭhena carāmi sabbaloke; “I travel the entire world with what I have gained, I wander the whole world on the reward [of my Awakening].“with what I have earned I wander the world.

Attho bhatiyā na vijjati, “I have no need of wages; No need for earnings is to be found:I have no need for wages:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Atthi vasā atthi dhenupā, “There is a cow and a suckling calf,” “There are cows, young bulls,“I have heifers and sucklings,”

(iti dhaniyo gopo) said Dhaniya the Cowherd, (Dhaniya the cattleman)said Dhaniya,

Godharaṇiyo paveṇiyopi atthi; “There is the herd-mother with her offspring, cows in calf, & breeding cows,“cows in calf and breeding cows.

Usabhopi gavampatīdha atthi, “The father-bull as well; & a great bull, the leader of the herd:I’ve also got a bull, captain of the herd here:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Natthi vasā natthi dhenupā, “There is no cow and no suckling calf,” “There are no cows, no young bulls,“I have no heifers or sucklings,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Godharaṇiyo paveṇiyopi natthi; “No herd-mother and no offspring, no cows in calf or breeding cows,“no cows in calf or breeding cows.

Usabhopi gavampatīdha natthi, “And no father-bull as well; no great bull, the leader of the herd:I haven’t got a bull, captain of the herd here:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Khilā nikhātā asampavedhī, “The post is firmly entrenched and does not wobble,” “The stakes are dug-in, immovable.“The stakes are driven in, unshakable,”

(iti dhaniyo gopo) said Dhaniya the Cowherd, (Dhaniya the cattleman)said Dhaniya,

Dāmā muñjamayā navā susaṇṭhānā; “The ropes are made of new and well-made twine, The new muñja-grass halters, well-woven,“The grass halters are new and well-woven,

Na hi sakkhinti dhenupāpi chettuṁ, “It is not possible for the calves to break them; not even young bulls could break:not even the sucklings can break them:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

“Usabhoriva chetva bandhanāni, “Like a bull that has broken its bonds,” “Having broken my bonds like a great bull,“Like a bull I broke the bonds,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Nāgo pūtilataṁva dālayitvā; “Like an elephant that has burst through rotten vines, like a great elephant tearing a rotting vine,“like an elephant I snapped the vine.

Nāhaṁ punupessaṁ gabbhaseyyaṁ, “I will never again enter a womb; I never again will lie in the womb:I will never lie in a womb again:

Atha ce patthayasī pavassa deva”. “So if the angels wish, they can make it rain.” So if you want, rain-god, go ahead & rain.”so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.”

Ninnañca thalañca pūrayanto, Flooding both the low ground and the high ground, filling the lowlands & high,Right then a vast cloud rained forth,

Mahāmegho pavassi tāvadeva; a great raincloud poured down rain. The great cloud rained down straightaway,soaking the uplands and valleys.

Sutvā devassa vassato, After hearing the rain from the angels, Hearing the rain-god pour down,Hearing the heavens rain down,

Imamatthaṁ dhaniyo abhāsatha. Dhaniya spoke about this: Dhaniya said:Dhaniya said this:

“Lābhā vata no anappakā, “It is a gain for us, and not a small one, “How great our gain“It is no small gain for us

Ye mayaṁ bhagavantaṁ addasāma; “That we have seen the Buddha; that we’ve gazed on the Blessed One!that we have seen the Buddha.

Saraṇaṁ taṁ upema cakkhuma, “I take refuge in the Seer, We go to him, the One with Eyes, for refuge.We come to you for refuge, Clear-eyed One.

Satthā no hohi tuvaṁ mahāmuni. “Great Sage, be our teacher. May you be our teacher, Great Sage.O great sage, please be our Teacher.

Gopī ca ahañca assavā, “I am a cow-herd, and I listen, My wife & I are composed.My wife and I, obedient,

Brahmacariyaṁ sugate carāmase; “I will live the spiritual life following the Sublime One, Let’s follow the holy life under the One Well-Gone.shall lead the spiritual life under the Holy One.

Jātimaraṇassa pāragū, “To go beyond birth and death, Gone to the far shore of aging & death,Gone beyond birth and death,

Dukkhassantakarā bhavāmase”. “And bring suffering to an end.” let’s put an end to suffering & stress.”we shall make an end of suffering.”

“Nandati puttehi puttimā, “One who has children delights because of their children,” “Those with children delight because of their children.“Children bring you delight!”

(iti māro pāpimā) said Māra the Evil One, (Māra the Evil One)said Māra the Wicked,

Gomā gohi tatheva nandati; “One who has cattle delights because of their cattle, Those with cattle delight because of their cows.“Cattle also bring you delight!

Upadhī hi narassa nandanā, “Possessions are the delight of a person, A person’s delight comes from acquisitions,For attachments are a man’s delight;

Na hi so nandati yo nirūpadhi”. “One who has no possessions has no delight.” for a person with no acquisitions doesn’t delight.”without attachments there’s no delight.”

“Socati puttehi puttimā, “One who has children sorrows because of their children,” “Those with children grieve precisely because of their children.“Your children bring you sorrow,”

(iti bhagavā) said the Buddha, (the Buddha)said the Buddha,

Gomā gohi tatheva socati; “One who has cattle sorrows because of their cattle, Those with cattle grieve precisely because of their cows.“Your cattle also bring you sorrow.

Upadhī hi narassa socanā, “Possessions are the the sorrow of a person, A person’s grief comes from acquisitions,For attachments are a man’s sorrow;

Na hi so socati yo nirūpadhī”ti. “One who has no possessions has no sorrow.” for a person with no acquisitions doesn’t grieve.”without attachments there are no sorrows.”

Dhaniyasuttaṁ dutiyaṁ. The Dhaniya Sutta, the second.
