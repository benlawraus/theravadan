Sutta Nipāta 5.14 Anthology of Discourses 5.14 Sutta Nipāta 5.13

Udayamāṇavapucchā The Questions of Udaya Udaya’s Questions

“Jhāyiṁ virajamāsīnaṁ, “To the meditator, rid of hopes,” “To the one in jhāna—seated, dustless,

(iccāyasmā udayo) said Venerable Udaya, (Udaya)

Katakiccaṁ anāsavaṁ; “who has completed the task, is free of defilements, passionless, his task done, effluent-free,

Pāraguṁ sabbadhammānaṁ, and has gone beyond all things, gone to the beyond of all phenomena—

Atthi pañhena āgamaṁ; I have come seeking with a question. I’ve come with a desire for a question.

Aññāvimokkhaṁ pabrūhi, Tell me the liberation by enlightenment, Tell me the gnosis of emancipation,

Avijjāya pabhedanaṁ”. the smashing of ignorance.” the breaking open of ignorance.”

“Pahānaṁ kāmacchandānaṁ, “The giving up of both” “The abandoning both of sensual desires,

(udayāti bhagavā) replied the Buddha, (the Buddha)

Domanassāna cūbhayaṁ; “sensual desires and displeasures; & of unhappiness,

Thinassa ca panūdanaṁ, the casting aside of dullness, the dispelling of sloth,

Kukkuccānaṁ nivāraṇaṁ. and the prevention of remorse; the warding off of anxieties,

Upekkhāsatisaṁsuddhaṁ, pure equanimity and mindfulness, equanimity-&-mindfulness purified,

dhammatakkapurejavaṁ; preceded by investigation of principles—with inspection of mental qualities swift in the forefront:

Aññāvimokkhaṁ pabrūmi, this, I declare, is liberation by enlightenment, That I call the gnosis of emancipation,

avijjāya pabhedanaṁ”. the smashing of ignorance.” the breaking open of ignorance.”

“Kiṁsu saṁyojano loko, “What fetters the world? “With what is the world fettered?

kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ; How does it travel about? With what is it examined?

Kissassa vippahānena, With the giving up of what Through the abandoning of what

nibbānaṁ iti vuccati”. is extinguishment spoken of?” is there said to be unbinding?”

“Nandisaṁyojano loko, “Delight fetters the world. “With delight the world’s fettered.

vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ; It travels about by means of thought. With directed thought it’s examined.

Taṇhāya vippahānena, With the giving up of craving Through the abandoning of craving

nibbānaṁ iti vuccati”. extinguishment is spoken of.” is there said to be unbinding.”

“Kathaṁ satassa carato, “For one living mindfully, “Living mindful in what way

viññāṇaṁ uparujjhati; how does consciousness cease? does one bring consciousness to a halt?

Bhagavantaṁ puṭṭhumāgamma, We’ve come to ask the worthy sir: We’ve come to ask the Blessed One.

taṁ suṇoma vaco tava”. let us hear what you say.” Let us hear your words.”

“Ajjhattañca bahiddhā ca, “Not taking pleasure in feeling “Not relishing feeling,

Vedanaṁ nābhinandato; internally and externally—inside or out:

Evaṁ satassa carato, for one living mindfully, One living mindful in this way

Viññāṇaṁ uparujjhatī”ti. that’s how consciousness ceases.” brings consciousness to a halt.”

Udayamāṇavapucchā terasamā. Udaya’s Questions, the thirteenth.
