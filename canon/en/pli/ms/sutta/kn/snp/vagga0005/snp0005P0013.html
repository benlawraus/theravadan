Sutta Nipāta 5.13 Anthology of Discourses 5.13 Sutta Nipāta 5.12

Bhadrāvudhamāṇavapucchā The Questions of Bhadrāvudha Bhadrāvudha’s Question

“Okañjahaṁ taṇhacchidaṁ anejaṁ, “I beseech you, the bastion-leaver, the craving-cutter, the imperturbable,” “I entreat the one who has abandoned home—cutting through craving, unperturbed;

(iccāyasmā bhadrāvudho) said Venerable Bhadrāvudha, (Bhadrāvudha)

Nandiñjahaṁ oghatiṇṇaṁ vimuttaṁ; “the delight-leaver, the flood-crosser, the freed, abandoned delight—crossed over the flood, released;

Kappañjahaṁ abhiyāce sumedhaṁ, the formulation-leaver, the intelligent. abandoned theory—very intelligent:

Sutvāna nāgassa apanamissanti ito. Different people have gathered from across the nations Having heard the Nāga, they will leave—

Nānājanā janapadehi saṅgatā, wishing to hear your word, O hero. the many gathered from many lands, hero,

Tava vīra vākyaṁ abhikaṅkhamānā; After hearing the spiritual giant they will depart from here. in hopes of your words.

Tesaṁ tuvaṁ sādhu viyākarohi, Please, sage, answer them clearly, So tell them, please,

Tathā hi te vidito esa dhammo”. for truly you understand this matter.” how this Dhamma has been known to you.”

“Ādānataṇhaṁ vinayetha sabbaṁ, “Dispel all craving for attachments,” “Subdue craving & grasping—all—

(bhadrāvudhāti bhagavā replied the Buddha, (the Buddha)

Uddhaṁ adho tiriyañcāpi majjhe; “above, below, all round, between. above, below, across, in between.

Yaṁ yañhi lokasmimupādiyanti, For whatever a personage grasps in the world, For whatever people cling to in the world,

Teneva māro anveti jantuṁ. Māra pursues them right there. it’s through that that Māra pursues them.

Tasmā pajānaṁ na upādiyetha, So let a mindful mendicant who understands So a monk, mindful, should cling to nothing

Bhikkhu sato kiñcanaṁ sabbaloke; not grasp anything in all the world, in all the world, every world,

Ādānasatte iti pekkhamāno, observing that, in clinging to attachments, seeing these people entangled in grasping

Pajaṁ imaṁ maccudheyye visattan”ti. these people cling to the domain of death.” as entangled in Death’s realm.”

Bhadrāvudhamāṇavapucchā dvādasamā. Bhadrāvudha’s Questions, the twelfth.
