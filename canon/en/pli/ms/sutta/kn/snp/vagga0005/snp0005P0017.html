Sutta Nipāta 5.17 Anthology of Discourses 5.17 Sutta Nipāta 5.16

Piṅgiyamāṇavapucchā The Questions of Piṅgiya Piṅgiya’s Questions

“Jiṇṇohamasmi abalo vītavaṇṇo, “I am old, feeble, and pallid,” “I’m old & weak, my complexion dull,

(iccāyasmā piṅgiyo) said Venerable Piṅgiya, (Piṅgiya)

Nettā na suddhā savanaṁ na phāsu; “my eyes unclear, my hearing faint—I’ve blurry eyes and trouble hearing—

Māhaṁ nassaṁ momuho antarāva, don’t let me perish while still confused. but may I not perish while still deluded, confused!

Ācikkha dhammaṁ yamahaṁ vijaññaṁ; Explain the teaching so that I may understand Teach me the Dhamma so that I may know

Jātijarāya idha vippahānaṁ”. the giving up of rebirth and old age here.” the abandoning here of birth & aging.”

“Disvāna rūpesu vihaññamāne, “Seeing them being harmed on account of forms,” “Seeing people suffering on account of their bodies—

(piṅgiyāti bhagavā) replied the Buddha, (the Buddha)

Ruppanti rūpesu janā pamattā; “those negligent folk are inflicted on account of forms—heedless people are afflicted on account of their bodies—

Tasmā tuvaṁ piṅgiya appamatto, therefore, Piṅgiya, being diligent, then heedful, Piṅgiya,

Jahassu rūpaṁ apunabbhavāya”. give up form so as not to be reborn.” let go of the body for the sake of no further becoming.”

“Disā catasso vidisā catasso, “The four quarters, the intermediate directions, “In the four cardinal directions, the four intermediate,

Uddhaṁ adho dasa disā imāyo; below, and above: in these ten directions above & below —these ten directions—

Na tuyhaṁ adiṭṭhaṁ asutaṁ amutaṁ, there’s nothing at all in the world there is nothing in the world unseen, unheard,

Atho aviññātaṁ kiñcanamatthi loke; that you’ve not seen, heard, thought, or cognized. unsensed, uncognized by you.

Ācikkha dhammaṁ yamahaṁ vijaññaṁ, Explain the teaching so that I may understand Teach me the Dhamma so that I may know

Jātijarāya idha vippahānaṁ”. the giving up of rebirth and old age here.” the abandoning here of birth & aging.”

“Taṇhādhipanne manuje pekkhamāno, “Observing mankind sunk in craving,” “Seeing people, victims of craving—

(piṅgiyāti bhagavā) replied the Buddha, (the Buddha)

Santāpajāte jarasā parete; “tormented, mired in old age; inflamed, overwhelmed with aging—

Tasmā tuvaṁ piṅgiya appamatto, therefore, Piṅgiya, being diligent, then heedful, Piṅgiya,

Jahassu taṇhaṁ apunabbhavāyā”ti. give up craving so as not to be reborn.” let go of craving for the sake of no further becoming.”

Piṅgiyamāṇavapucchā soḷasamā. Piṅgiya’s Questions, the sixteenth.
