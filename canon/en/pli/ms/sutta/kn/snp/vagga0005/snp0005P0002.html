Sutta Nipāta 5.2 Sutta Nipāta 5.2Anthology of Discourses 5.2

Ajitamāṇavapucchā Ajita’s QuestionsThe Questions of Ajita

“Kenassu nivuto loko, “With what is the world shrouded?“By what is the world shrouded?”

(iccāyasmā ajito) (Ajita)said Venerable Ajita.

Kenassu nappakāsati; Because of what doesn’t it shine?“Why does it not shine?

Kissābhilepanaṁ brūsi, With what is it smeared? Tell me.Tell me, what is its sullying?

Kiṁsu tassa mahabbhayaṁ”. What is its great danger & fear?”What is its greatest fear?”

“Avijjāya nivuto loko, “With ignorance the world is shrouded.“The world is shrouded in ignorance.”

(ajitāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)replied the Buddha.

Vevicchā pamādā nappakāsati; Because of stinginess, heedlessness, it doesn’t shine.“Avarice and negligence make it not shine.

Jappābhilepanaṁ brūmi, With longing it’s smeared—I tell you.Muttered prayer is its sullying, I say.

Dukkhamassa mahabbhayaṁ”. Suffering-stress: its great danger & fear.”Suffering is its greatest fear.”

“Savanti sabbadhi sotā, “They flow every which way, the streams.“The streams flow everywhere,”

(iccāyasmā ajito) (Ajita)said Venerable Ajita.

Sotānaṁ kiṁ nivāraṇaṁ; What is their blocking,“What is there to block them?

Sotānaṁ saṁvaraṁ brūhi, what their restraint—tell me—And tell me the restraint of streams—

Kena sotā pidhiyyare”. with what are they finally stopped?”by what are they locked out?”

“Yāni sotāni lokasmiṁ, “Whatever streams there are in the world:“The streams in the world,”

(ajitāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)replied the Buddha,

Sati tesaṁ nivāraṇaṁ; Their blocking is mindfulness,“are blocked by mindfulness.

Sotānaṁ saṁvaraṁ brūmi, mindfulness is their restraint—I tell you—I tell you the restraint of streams—

Paññāyete pidhiyyare”. with discernment they’re finally stopped.”they are locked out by wisdom.”

“Paññā ceva sati yañca, “Discernment & mindfulness,“That wisdom and mindfulness,”

(iccāyasmā ajito) (Ajita)said Venerable Ajita,

Nāmarūpañca mārisa; name-&-form, dear sir:“and that which is name and form, good fellow;

Etaṁ me puṭṭho pabrūhi, Tell me, when asked this,when questioned, please tell me of this:

Katthetaṁ uparujjhati”. where are they brought to a halt?”where does this all cease?”

“Yametaṁ pañhaṁ apucchi, “This question you’ve asked, Ajita,“This question which you have asked,

ajita taṁ vadāmi te; I’ll answer it for you—I shall answer you, Ajita.

Yattha nāmañca rūpañca, where name-&-formWhere name and form

asesaṁ uparujjhati; are brought to a halt without trace:cease with no residue left behind—

Viññāṇassa nirodhena, With the cessation of consciousnesswith the cessation of consciousness,

etthetaṁ uparujjhati”. they’re here brought to a halt.”here they cease.”

“Ye ca saṅkhātadhammāse, “Those here who have fathomed the Dhamma,“There are those who have appraised the teaching,

ye ca sekhā puthū idha; those who are learners, those who are run-of-the-mill:and many kinds of trainees here.

Tesaṁ me nipako iriyaṁ, When you, dear sir, astute,Now you, being alert, are asked

puṭṭho pabrūhi mārisa”. are asked this, tell me their manner of life.”to tell me their behavior, good fellow.”

“Kāmesu nābhigijjheyya, “He should not hanker for sensual pleasures,“Not greedy for sensual pleasures,

Manasānāvilo siyā; should be limpid in mind.their mind would be unclouded.

Kusalo sabbadhammānaṁ, Skilled in all mental qualities,Skilled in all things,

Sato bhikkhu paribbaje”ti. he, the monk, should wander mindfully.”a mendicant would wander mindful.”

Ajitamāṇavapucchā paṭhamā. Ajita’s Questions, the first.
