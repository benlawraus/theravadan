Sutta Nipāta 5.7 Sutta Nipāta 5.6Anthology of Discourses 5.7

Upasīvamāṇavapucchā Upasīva’s QuestionsThe Questions of Upasīva

“Eko ahaṁ sakka mahantamoghaṁ, “Alone, Sakyan, with nothing to rely on,“Alone and independent, O Sakyan,”

(iccāyasmā upasīvo) (Upasīva)said Venerable Upasīva,

Anissito no visahāmi tārituṁ; I can’t venture across the great flood.“I am not able to cross the great flood.

Ārammaṇaṁ brūhi samantacakkhu, Tell me, All-around Eye,Tell me a support, All-seer,

Yaṁ nissito oghamimaṁ tareyyaṁ”. the support to rely on for crossing over this flood.”depending on which I may cross this flood.”

“Ākiñcaññaṁ pekkhamāno satimā, “Mindfully focused on nothingness,“Mindfully contemplating nothingness,”

(upasīvāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)replied the Buddha,

Natthīti nissāya tarassu oghaṁ; relying on ‘There isn’t,’ you should cross over the flood.“depending on the perception ‘there is nothing’, cross the flood.

Kāme pahāya virato kathāhi, Abandoning sensuality, abstaining from conversations,Giving up sensual pleasures, refraining from chatter,

Taṇhakkhayaṁ nattamahābhipassa”. keep watch for the ending of craving, night & day.”watch day and night for the ending of craving.”

“Sabbesu kāmesu yo vītarāgo, “One free from passion for all sensuality“One who is free of desire for sensual pleasures,”

(iccāyasmā upasīvo) (Upasīva)said Venerable Upasīva,

Ākiñcaññaṁ nissito hitvā maññaṁ; relying on nothingness, letting go of all else,“depending on nothingness, all else left behind,

Saññāvimokkhe parame vimutto, released in the highest emancipation of perception:freed in the ultimate liberation of perception:

Tiṭṭhe nu so tattha anānuyāyī”. Does he stay there unaffected?”might they remain there without traveling on?”

“Sabbesu kāmesu yo vītarāgo, “One free from passion for all sensuality“One free of desire for sensual pleasures,”

(upasīvāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)replied the Buddha,

Ākiñcaññaṁ nissito hitvā maññaṁ; relying on nothingness, letting go of all else,“depending on nothingness, all else left behind,

Saññāvimokkhe parame vimutto, released in the highest emancipation of perception:freed in the ultimate liberation of perception:

Tiṭṭheyya so tattha anānuyāyī”. He stays there unaffected.”they might remain there without traveling on.”

“Tiṭṭhe ce so tattha anānuyāyī, “If, All-around Eye, he stays there,“If they were to remain there without traveling on,

Pūgampi vassānaṁ samantacakkhu; unaffected for many years,even for myriad years, All-seer,

Tattheva so sītisiyā vimutto, right there would he be cooled & released?and, being freed, were to grow cool right there,

Cavetha viññāṇaṁ tathāvidhassa”. Would his consciousness be like that?”would the consciousness of that sort pass away?”

“Accī yathā vātavegena khittā, “As a flame overthrown by the force of the wind“As a flame tossed by a gust of wind,”

(upasīvāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)replied the Buddha,

Atthaṁ paleti na upeti saṅkhaṁ; goes to an end that cannot be classified,“comes to an end and cannot be reckoned;

Evaṁ munī nāmakāyā vimutto, so the sage freed from the name-bodyso too, a sage freed from the set of mental phenomena

Atthaṁ paleti na upeti saṅkhaṁ”. goes to an end that cannot be classified.”comes to an end and cannot be reckoned.”

“Atthaṅgato so uda vā so natthi, “One who has reached the end: Does he not exist,“One who has disappeared—do they not exist?

Udāhu ve sassatiyā arogo; or is he for eternity free from dis-ease?Or are they free from disease for eternity?

Taṁ me munī sādhu viyākarohi, Please, sage, declare this to mePlease, sage, answer me clearly,

Tathā hi te vidito esa dhammo”. as this phenomenon has been known by you.”for truly you understand this matter.”

“Atthaṅgatassa na pamāṇamatthi, “One who has reached the end has no criterion“One who has disappeared cannot be defined,”

(upasīvāti bhagavā) (the Buddha)replied the Buddha.

Yena naṁ vajjuṁ taṁ tassa natthi; by which anyone would say that—for him it doesn’t exist.“They have nothing by which others might describe them.

Sabbesu dhammesu samohatesu, When all phenomena are done away with,When all things have been eradicated,

Samūhatā vādapathāpi sabbe”ti. all means of speaking are done away with as well.”eradicated, too, are all ways of speech.”

Upasīvamāṇavapucchā chaṭṭhī. Upasīva’s Questions, the sixth.
