Sutta Nipāta 5.12 Anthology of Discourses 5.12 Sutta Nipāta 5.11

Jatukaṇṇimāṇavapucchā The Questions of Jatukaṇṇī Jatukaṇṇin’s Question

“Sutvānahaṁ vīramakāmakāmiṁ, “Hearing of the hero with no desire for sensual pleasures,” “Hearing, hero, of one with no desire for sensuality,

(iccāyasmā jatukaṇṇi) said Venerable Jatukaṇṇī, (Jatukaṇṇin)

Oghātigaṁ puṭṭhumakāmamāgamaṁ; “who has passed over the flood, I’ve come with a question for that desireless one. I’ve come to ask the one gone beyond the flood, sensuality-free:

Santipadaṁ brūhi sahajanetta, Tell me the state of peace, O natural visionary. Tell me the state of peace, Blessed One, Simultaneous Eye.

Yathātacchaṁ bhagavā brūhi metaṁ. Tell me this, Blessed One, as it really is. Tell me as it actually is.

Bhagavā hi kāme abhibhuyya iriyati, For, having mastered sensual desires, the Blessed One proceeds, For the Blessed One lives having conquered sensuality,

Ādiccova pathaviṁ tejī tejasā; as the blazing sun shines on the earth. as the radiant sun, in its radiance, the earth.

Parittapaññassa me bhūripañña, May you of vast wisdom explain the teaching Limited my discernment, O Deeply Discerning.

Ācikkha dhammaṁ yamahaṁ vijaññaṁ; to me of little wisdom so that I may understand Teach me to know the Dhamma,

Jātijarāya idha vippahānaṁ”. the giving up of rebirth and old age here.” the abandoning here of birth & aging.”

“Kāmesu vinaya gedhaṁ, “With desire for sensual pleasures dispelled,” “Subdue greed for sensuality,

(jatukaṇṇīti bhagavā) replied the Buddha, (the Buddha)

Nekkhammaṁ daṭṭhu khemato; “seeing renunciation as sanctuary, & see renunciation as rest.

Uggahītaṁ nirattaṁ vā, don’t be taking up or putting down Let there be nothing grasped

Mā te vijjittha kiñcanaṁ. anything at all. or rejected by you.

Yaṁ pubbe taṁ visosehi, What came before, let wither away, Burn up what’s before,

pacchā te māhu kiñcanaṁ; and after, let there be nothing. and have nothing for after.

Majjhe ce no gahessasi, If you don’t grasp at the middle, If you don’t grasp at what’s in between,

upasanto carissasi. you will live at peace. you will go about, calm.

Sabbaso nāmarūpasmiṁ, One rid of greed, brahmin, One completely devoid of greed

Vītagedhassa brāhmaṇa; for the whole realm of name and form, for name-&-form, brahman,

Āsavāssa na vijjanti, has no defilements by which has no effluents

Yehi maccuvasaṁ vaje”ti. they might fall under the sway of Death.” by which he would go under Māra’s sway.”

Jatukaṇṇimāṇavapucchā ekādasamā. Jatukaṇṇin’s Questions, the eleventh.
