Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikadukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (4)

16.58. Sanidassanattika, Āsavaduka

Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ āsavaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho noāsavo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ āsavaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho noāsavo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā …pe… anidassanaappaṭighaṁ āsavaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho noāsavo ca naanidassanasappaṭigho noāsavo ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, ārammaṇe tīṇi …pe… avigate cha.
