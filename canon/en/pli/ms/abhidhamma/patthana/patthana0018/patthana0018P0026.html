Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Dukadukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (6)

18.26. Saraṇaduka, Cūḷantaraduka

Araṇo appaccayo dhammo nasaraṇassa naappaccayassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

Ārammaṇe adhipatiyā upanissaye ekaṁ.

Araṇo asaṅkhato dhammo nasaraṇassa naasaṅkhatassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

Ārammaṇe adhipatiyā upanissaye ekaṁ.

Araṇo sanidassano dhammo naaraṇassa nasanidassanassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. Araṇo sanidassano dhammo nasaraṇassa nasanidassanassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

Ārammaṇe dve, adhipatiyā ekaṁ, upanissaye purejāte atthiyā avigate dve.
